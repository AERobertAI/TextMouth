## Why
The user listens for hours with VoiceOver and AirPods; a stall that needs a quit, a few words skipped, or the AirPods reaching the wrong book is what they notice. These are the open items left by the speech-refusal follow-ups (October 7 evening), the Now Playing follow-ups (October 10), B05, and FR12's branch C, each in the TODO's "Still open after the … merge" lines; read those lines and the agents' notes in `AGENT-NOTES-2026-10-04.md` (speechfix, speechguard, refusalalert, nowplaying, fullsummary) first.

A standing rule of the user's (from an earlier session, "Playback architecture constraint"): never switch from `AVSpeechSynthesizer` direct speech to the audio engine's rendered audio while speech is being heard. The position the synthesizer reports runs ahead of the audio, so any such switch repeats or skips words. Switch only at natural breaks: a seek, a pause and resume, a chapter's start, or a recovery from speech that has already stopped.

## Items, one commit each, each with its tests
1. **The sleep fade's switch.** `SpeechService.setSleepTimerFadeVolume(_:duration:)` switches direct speech to rendered audio as the fade begins (`SLEEP_FADE_SWITCH: direct→engine`), the one switch made while speech is heard, so a few words can be skipped. While direct speech is heard, fade with the direct utterance fade (`prepareDirectSleepFade`, `DirectSpeechFadePlanner`), which already serves when the engine fails; keep the engine fade for speech already playing from rendered audio. Test that no fade switches backends while direct speech plays.
2. **A seek that cannot start the engine.** `seek(toCharOffset:)` leaves speech in neither backend while it shows speaking when the engine fails to start; fall back to direct speech as the other switches do.
3. **An utterance that never starts.** An utterance the synthesizer neither starts nor cancels is not watched, since the watchdog arms at a start: arm a start watchdog when an utterance is handed over, recovering as for an idle synthesizer. Tests with the fake synthesizers the existing watchdog tests use; no test sleeps (pass the clock or timer in, as they do).
4. **The retry's place.** The record of a retry's place (`lastDirectRetryOffset`) lasts until speech stops, so a later refusal at that exact offset, after speech has moved on, ends speech without its one retry. Clear it once speech has moved past the place.
5. **A refused chapter render.** A chapter render the engine refuses (`RENDER_REFUSED`) is never retried strictly guarded, so that chapter has no rendered audio to switch to; retry it once, strictly guarded, as direct speech's retry does.
6. **#1141.** `DirectSpeechFadePlanner.segments` still holds the slow type-check expression; split it so it type-checks quickly, with its tests unchanged.
7. **Now Playing leftovers.** `pause(playingPauseSound:)` tells Now Playing "paused" even when it paused nothing, so a pause reaching a stopped reader takes the AirPods: publish only when it paused something or the reader holds the media controls. `showEditedMetadata` (`ReaderViewModel+LibraryUpdates.swift`) guards with playing or paused, so a finished book holding the controls keeps its old title after a rename: use `holdsMediaControls`. `clearNowPlaying(ownerID:)` takes nothing down for a reader shown but not active, as a stop from behind another window: take its own item down, as closing now does.
