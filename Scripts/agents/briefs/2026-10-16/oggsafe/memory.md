## The problem
On October 14, 2026 Apple's Vorbis decoder, stuck inside AudioToolbox, grew TextMouth to 34 GB in about nine minutes (about 60 MB a second), and the Mac had to be reset. `PlaybackMemoryGuard` (B07) reads the footprint only while some reader plays and is not paused: `MemoryGuardPolicy.isWatching` (:81-83) and `MemoryWatch.takeTheNextReading` (:475-480) schedule no reading otherwise, and `MemoryGuardPolicy.act` (:87-100) never quits unless something plays or a stopped runaway is still under way. So when the stall watchdog fails a track, or the listener presses Pause, while a wedged thread keeps allocating under 3 GB, nothing watches, and the 6 GB quit can never fire. The same holds if a wedge starts when nothing has played at all (an import).

## Required behavior
1. **The quit ceiling acts whether or not anything plays.** `MemoryGuardPolicy.act(onFootprint:isAnythingPlaying:)` returns `.saveAndQuit` at or above `quitCeiling` in every state. The stop ceiling still acts only while something plays: at or above `stopCeiling` with nothing playing and no runaway stopped, it returns `.none`; a stop sets `hasStoppedPlayback` as today, and a footprint back under the stop ceiling clears it.
2. **The guard always reads.** Once begun, it schedules the next reading after every reading, until it begins to quit. The wait is `readInterval` (5 s) while anything plays, while a stopped runaway goes on, or while the last footprint read was at or above a new `MemoryGuardPolicy.watchCloselyFootprint` of 1 GB (at 60 MB a second, 30 s from under 1 GB reaches 2.8 GB, below the stop ceiling, so no reading is missed that matters); otherwise the new `Environment.idleReadInterval` of 30 s. Decide the wait in the policy, from the state and the last reading, so it is testable without a clock; document the numbers and the reasoning in the comments.
3. **Beginning.** New `func beginWatching()` on `PlaybackMemoryGuard` (and the static convenience the lead can call as `PlaybackMemoryGuard.shared.beginWatching()`): starts the loop if the guard `isEnabled` and none runs; calling it twice starts one loop. `reader(_:isPlaying:title:)` begins the loop too, as it does today. The app's `shared` guard is disabled in hosted tests (`isEnabled` false), so nothing in the suite starts a real loop.
4. **The marker and the words when nothing played.** `MemoryQuitMarker` gains a flag for "nothing was playing and no playback had been stopped for this runaway when TextMouth quit"; the quit's `bookTitle` and the policy's state decide it. An older marker file (no such key) must still decode and read as before, and the existing tests that build `MemoryQuitMarker(date:footprint:bookTitle:)` must compile and compare equal unchanged (give the new property a default). The announcement, read by VoiceOver in an alert at the next launch, is for an idle quit: "TextMouth quit on October 14, 2026 at 5:03 PM to protect your Mac: its memory grew to 6.1 GB while nothing was playing. Your place was saved." (same date style as now); the playing wording stays exactly as it is.
5. Tests drive the guard with `readNow()` as today. `makeGuard()` must set `idleReadInterval` to 3,600 like `readInterval` so no test guard reads on its own, except in the new loop test below.

## New tests (in `PlaybackMemoryGuardTests`)
- Policy: the quit ceiling quits with nothing playing, before and after a stop, and at once; the stop ceiling does not stop with nothing playing; the wait is 5 s while playing, while a runaway goes on, at 1 GB and above, and 30 s otherwise, and returns to 30 s once the footprint falls back.
- Guard: a reader that plays and then pauses (or stops) under 1 GB, then the dial set to 6 GB and `readNow()`, quits (marker, save, quit, in order, as `testTheSecondCeilingAfterTheStopSavesEverythingAndQuits` records); a guard that never saw a reader quits at 7 GB; an idle quit's marker carries the idle flag and a playing one does not.
- Loop: a guard built with `readInterval` and `idleReadInterval` of 0.05 s, `beginWatching()` called twice, nothing ever playing, the dial raised to 7 GB: the quit is recorded within a few seconds (use the file's `eventually`), and the readings stop after it (the dial counts reads; no read after the quit begins).
- Marker: the idle announcement words above, exactly; a JSON marker without the new key decodes and announces in the playing words; a round trip keeps the flag.
