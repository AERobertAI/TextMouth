## ST07 (to `stable020-launch`): `AsyncBridge.waitForResult` has no time limit
**The bug** (§3.2 item 7). `AsyncBridge.waitForResult` (`TextMouth/Services/AsyncBridge.swift:30-66`) waits on a semaphore with no limit. An AVFoundation read that never returns leaves a reader on Loading, or a single-file import stuck with no cancel. The main thread stays free.

**Do:**
1. Add a time limit to the bridge: `waitForResult(timeLimit: Duration? = nil, cancelledBy: ImportCancellation? = nil, of:)`, keeping the two existing signatures as they are (no limit). With a limit, the wait is `finished.wait(timeout:)`; when it times out, the detached task is cancelled and `AsyncBridge.TimeLimitExceeded` is thrown, a `LocalizedError` holding the limit, whose `errorDescription` is `"Reading the audio took longer than <limit>, so TextMouth stopped waiting for it."`, the limit spelled "30 seconds" or "5 minutes" (whole minutes when it is a whole number of minutes, else seconds; one minute is "1 minute"). The task, left to finish on its own, stores its late result where nobody reads it; say so in a comment. One `DiagnosticLog.event(.playback, "ASYNC_TIME_LIMIT: an audio read passed its limit of <limit> and was abandoned")` line, with no file name or title.
2. The limits, each a named constant on `AsyncBridge` with a doc comment saying why that long:
   - reading an audio file's tags (`ReaderViewModel.swift:1495`, `FileImportService.swift:253`): 30 seconds; the existing `try?` fallbacks give empty tags, as now;
   - analyzing an audiobook's audio (`BookContentAnalysisService.swift:559` and `:564`) and parsing one (`BookParser.swift:66`): 5 minutes; the error reaches the reader's or the import's failure message as any read error does.
   - The others (audio-edit renders, conversions, textutil) keep no limit; say why in the constant's comment: they have their own cancel or watchdog.
3. **Tests,** in the class that tests `AsyncBridge` (find it; if none, add `AsyncBridgeTests` with `xcodegen generate`, the committed `project.pbxproj`, and its line in `Scripts/test/areas.conf`): an operation that sleeps 60 seconds with a limit of 0.2 seconds throws `TimeLimitExceeded` within 2 seconds, and its task is cancelled (the operation sees cancellation); an operation that returns at once gives its value; the sentence for 30 seconds, for 300 seconds ("5 minutes"), and for 60 seconds ("1 minute"), pinned exactly. `LogContentPolicyTests` must pass with the new line.
