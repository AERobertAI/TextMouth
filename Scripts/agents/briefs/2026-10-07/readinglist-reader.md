## Context (verify as you go)
- A reader window is identified by its book's ID (`openWindow(id: "book-reader", value: book.id)`), so the reading-list context cannot ride on the window's value; it comes through a request the reader claims, as Continue to Next Book's autoplay does (`ReaderAutoplayRequests`, Services/ReaderAutoplayRequests.swift: a main-actor store, a request per book, an expiry, claimed once).
- The window's title is `BookReaderWindowView.windowTitle` (Views/Reader/BookReaderWindowView.swift): "Book: <title> by <author>", or "Book: <title>" when the author is unknown.
- The end of a book is `ReaderViewModel.handleEndOfBook()` (ViewModels/ReaderViewModel+Playback.swift about line 2003): stop, loop, or continue to the next book in the folder (`nextBookInFolder(after:)`, `continueToNextBook(_:)` with its announcement and delay), with the sleep timer's rules (`sleepTimerEndsListeningHere`, `expireSleepTimerAtChapterEnd`), `hasFinishedBook` (B02), and `markBookFinished`.
- The library answers `readingListContinuation(after:)` (the book to play next under the list's options, or nil for Stop, the end of the list, only finished books beyond, or a book no longer listed) and `readingListNeighbor(of:direction:skippingFinished:)`.
