### 1. A hand-over that lands on a finished book plays it (finding 5)
**The bug.** With "Skip finished" off (a folder's or feed's listening options, `EndOfBookRule.skipsFinished`, or the reading list's Skip Finished Books, `libraryViewModel.readingList.skipsFinished`) a book that ends can hand over to a book that rests at its end: `LibraryViewModel+Succession.swift:78` returns the finished row, and `ReaderViewModel+ReadingList.swift` does the same through the reading list. `handOverToNextBook` says "Starting: 2" and asks for book 2 (`continueToNextBook(_:)`, `ReaderViewModel+Playback.swift:2901`). Book 2's reader claims the request (`claimAutoplayRequest()`, :2928-2936; its two callers are `ReaderViewModel.swift:1218` when loading ends and :1548 when a request reaches an open reader), which calls `playOrResume()` → `play()`; book 2's cursor rests at its end, so `play()` takes `guard !cursorRestsAtEnd else { playFromTheEnd() }` (:48-51), which runs book 2's own end rule: "Starting: 3", and none of book 2 is heard. The existing test of the setting, `testSkipFinishedBooksPassesOverAFinishedBookBelowOrPlaysIt` (`ContinueToNextBookTests.swift:1086`), checks only that book 2 is asked for.
**The fix.** In `claimAutoplayRequest()`, once the request is claimed and before the book plays: when the reader rests at its end (`cursorRestsAtEnd`), move it back to its beginning first, exactly as Stop and Play Starts Over does (`returnToTheBeginning()`, :2635, private in this file: it moves the cursor to the start, clears `hasFinishedBook`, begins a new playback generation, and saves, so the row reads unfinished), and then play it from there. Say nothing more: "Starting: <title>" was said when the hand-over began. Read `playFromTheBeginning(announcing:)` (:2648) and decide whether to end with `play()` or `playOrResume()`: after `returnToTheBeginning()` an audiobook's player may still report paused at its old end, and `playOrResume()` would then resume it there; whichever you choose, a test must prove a finished audiobook (its player stopped at the end) and a finished text book both play from their beginnings. A reader that rests finished and is NOT claimed by a hand-over (the listener opens it and presses Play) still runs its end rule, as the user decided on October 10 (TODO P07): do not touch `play()` or `playFromTheEnd()`.
**Scope.** The reading list is covered by the same fix, since `continueThroughReadingList(to:)` (`ReaderViewModel+ReadingList.swift:146`) and Next in Reading List (`goThroughReadingList`, :193) make the same autoplay request; prove it with a test, do not edit `ReaderViewModel+ReadingList.swift`. Update the doc comment of `claimAutoplayRequest()` with the new rule.
**Tests.** In `ContinueToNextBookTests` (it has `makeFolderWorld(options:finished:)`, `endBook(...)`, and `world.requests`; add a section at the end of the class and use the same helpers), then the reading list's equivalent where its existing tests keep theirs (search `TextMouthProductionTests` for `readingListContinuation` and `skipsFinished`): (a) a folder of three books, the second finished, `ListeningOptions(continuation: .playBookBelow, skipsFinished: false)`: book 1 ends, "Starting: Second Volume" is said, a reader for book 2 (with `autoplayRequests: world.requests`) claims the request and plays from offset 0, `hasFinishedBook` is false, the row no longer rests at its end, and nothing more is announced, in particular no "Starting: Third Volume"; (b) the same where book 2's reader was already open and finished when the request arrived; (c) a finished audiobook (see `FinishedBookReaderTests` for how its fixtures and a private media session are built: `makeAudioBook`, `privateMediaSession()`); (d) the reading list with Skip Finished Books off; (e) with skipping on the finished book is still passed over (the existing test keeps passing); (f) a finished book opened by the listener who presses Play still runs its end rule (the existing `FinishedBookReaderTests` keep passing).
