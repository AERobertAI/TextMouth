## Commits (one each, each with its tests; build and test after each)

### 1. Later end decisions end the wait; the sleep timer refuses a late hand-over (finding 1)
The bug: the wait outlives every later end decision. Episode A ends: "Finished: A. Downloading the next episode: B." The listener starts a 30-minute sleep timer in A's window; Play runs `playFromTheEnd()`, which says "Finished: A. Next book not started because of the sleep timer."; B's download then finishes and B starts anyway, since `goOnWithNextEpisode` looks only at `restsFinishedAwaitingTheNextEpisode`. The same after the feed's options are set to Stop and Play is pressed. Required:
1. `goOnPastThisBook` ends any earlier wait as its first act (`nextEpisodeWait = nil`), in all its branches (nothing, book, episode to download: the last then starts the new wait as it does now), with a comment.
2. `playFromTheEnd()` ends any earlier wait as its first act too (its Stop, Starts Over, and Loop branches do not pass through `goOnPastThisBook`), with a comment.
3. `goOnWithNextEpisode` refuses while this reader's sleep timer is in effect (`sleepTimerEndsListeningHere`), after the "listener has moved on" check: it says what `goOnPastThisBook` says then, `EndOfBookWording.finished(book.title, notes: [EndOfBookWording.nextBookNotStartedNote])`, through the same helper (make `saySleepTimerKeepsNextBookFromStarting` non-private and call it, with `say: announceToVoiceOver(_:)`; no copy of the sentence), and hands nothing over.
Tests in `EpisodeDownloadContinuationTests` (`testTheSleepTimerKeepsTheNextEpisodeFromBeingDownloaded` at :182 shows how a shared sleep timer is started and put back): (a) the timer is started after `handleEndOfBook()` and the download then finishes: the announcements are the two `Finished` sentences and `heard.asked` is empty; (b) the timer is started and Play is pressed during the wait: "Finished: Middle part. Next book not started because of the sleep timer." is said, `nextEpisodeWait` is nil, and the arrival then hands nothing over and says nothing more; (c) the feed's options are changed to `ListeningOptions(continuation: .stop)` (`center.setListeningOptions(_:of:)`, as `makeFeed` does) during the wait and Play is pressed: "Finished: Middle part." is said and nothing is handed over when the download arrives; (d) a second end decision from `goOnPastThisBook` with nothing to follow clears the wait; (e) the normal case still hands over (the existing tests keep passing).

### 2. Another book's playing ends the wait (finding 2)
The bug: `restsFinishedAwaitingTheNextEpisode` looks at this reader alone, so a download that takes minutes still hands over after the listener has started another book (that book's `play()` pauses every other reader through `NowPlayingService.prepareForPlayback`, and B's start would pause it), and a failed download's "The next episode could not be downloaded, so playback stopped" is spoken over the other book. Required:
1. `NowPlayingSession` gets `var activeOwnerID: UUID?` (the coordinator's), with a doc comment.
2. `waitForNextEpisode` notes `nowPlayingService.activeOwnerID` as the wait begins (after the end has been said): the owner of the media controls as this episode ended, which may be nil or another reader's. Where the wait later hands over, or announces a failure or a cancel, it first checks the owner is still that one; if not, another reader has taken the controls (it plays, or its window became the focused one), the listener has moved on, and the wait ends silently with a trace line in the debug log (`DiagnosticLog.trace(.playback, ...)`, as `goOnWithNextEpisode` has). Fold this into one check with `restsFinishedAwaitingTheNextEpisode` (it takes the noted owner), so every path asks it once (DRY).
Tests: give `openReader(for:in:)` an optional `nowPlaying:` argument and use one private coordinator for two readers (`NowPlayingService(installsSystemIntegrations: false, diagnosticSink: { _ in })`, as `ContinueToNextBookTests` does); the second reader opens another downloaded book of the feed. (a) Reader A ends, the wait begins; the second reader takes the controls (`nowPlayingService.activateRemoteCommandsIfNeeded()`); the download finishes: nothing is handed over, nothing is said, `nextEpisodeWait` is nil. (b) The same with a failed download: "could not be downloaded" is not said. (c) The second reader's window merely becomes the focused one (`windowBecameKey()`): the same silence. (d) The controls were another reader's already as A ended and stay so: the hand-over happens. (e) A's own window becomes key again meanwhile: the hand-over happens.

### 3. The wait goes first in the queue, and a cancelled download is told (finding 3's call site, finding 6)
1. `waitForNextEpisode` asks with `feeds.download(item.id, of: feedID, startingNext: true)`. Test: with a download under way and another queued behind it, ending the episode puts the next episode first in `center.downloads.queue`; and a Play during the wait leaves it there (the fixture's feed has three episodes; add what you need to `makeFeed`, keeping the existing tests' behavior).
2. `nextEpisodeEventArrived` hears `.downloadCancelled(feedID, itemID)` for this wait's item: the wait ends (`nextEpisodeWait = nil`), and when the listener has not moved on (the same check) it says the user's words (question 19, October 16): **"Download cancelled, so playback stopped."** One new constant in `EndOfBookWording` beside `nextEpisodeNotDownloaded` (:110), one wording for an episode and an article both. Nothing is said when the listener moved on or closed the window.
Tests: pin the string in `testTheWords`; after `handleEndOfBook()`, `center.cancelDownload("guid:ep-1", of: feedID)` says it once and nothing is handed over; so does the downloads section's Cancel (`center.cancelEveryDownload()`); a cancel after the listener played again, or after `close()`, says nothing; a download that finishes after another cancelled one of a different item is not confused with it.
