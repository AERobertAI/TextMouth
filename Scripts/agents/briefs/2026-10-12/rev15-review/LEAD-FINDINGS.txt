L1  PlaceRelocator.swift:336-343 (relocate(_ skipped:)) + TextAnchorFinder.findHeading label fallback
    After the diff proves a skipped section was REMOVED (.removed -> labelRequired), the skip still goes to ANY other heading of its
    level with the same label anywhere in the book (findHeading's onlyHeadingWithItsLabel, or the nearestHeadingWithItsLabel guess).
    Probe lead/notes: chapters I and II each end with a level-2 "Notes" section; only chapter I's is skipped; Edit Text removes
    chapter I's Notes -> skip moves to chapter II's Notes (finding .foundBySavedWords(.onlyHeadingWithItsLabel)); with a third
    chapter's Notes too -> .guessed(.savedWords(.nearestHeadingWithItsLabel)) -> chapter II's Notes. Hides text never skipped.
