## The rule
At load, a skip whose heading does not stand names another heading of its level only on proof:
1. **Direct check** (unchanged): its saved words stand at its saved place, where a heading of its level with its label starts.
2. **Proof by the section's opening words**: every one of the skip's saved words after the heading (`Quote.after`) agrees, in order, from that heading's first word, and at least `sectionProofWords` (4, as many as a candidate spot needs, `seedWords`) of them lie past the label's words. The label's words are the leading run of the quote that agrees with the label's fine words, the longer of the skip's label and the candidate's: a label alone never proves a section, however long it is.
3. **One proven heading** is it (`.found(.savedWords(score:))`, the saved words' score there). Of several proven (sections that open alike), the one with the skip's label, when exactly one has it. Otherwise nil: the skip waits, kept in `waitingSkippedHeadings`.

Gone, at load and from the finder:
- the score that let the words before the heading carry a heading (F1a, F1b: a removed section's words before it now stand before the next section);
- the label bonus, and the label-alone fallback (`onlyHeadingWithItsLabel` at load; F1c: another chapter's "Notes");
- the guess (`nearestHeadingWithItsLabel`), which the load path refused already.

Why the whole quote after the heading, not a run of it: two sections that share a label and their first few words ("Notes / The pump belonged to the angling club" against "Notes / The pump belonged to the mill") must not stand in for each other. A heading that only moved (text added before it) keeps every word after it, so it still keeps its skip. What this gives up, knowingly: a section moved and edited within its first 64 units waits at load (a rebuild's diff still carries it), and a cover that moved waits (no words to prove it), as every such skip did before branch E.
