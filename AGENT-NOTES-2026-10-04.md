  - The reader settles that pause with the same steps as a refusal's stop; both now go through one function, `settleStopMadeBySpeech()`.
  - Log lines: `DIRECT_START_STALLED` (offsets only) and `PLAYBACK_PAUSED_AFTER_START_STALLS` (chapter and offset).
