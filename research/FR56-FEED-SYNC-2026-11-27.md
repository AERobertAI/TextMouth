# FR56: syncing feeds between devices

A design, November 27, 2026, for FR56, built on the library sync (FR43, research/CLOUD-SYNC-BUILD-PLAN-2026-11-25.md).
Where this design and the code differ, the code wins.

## What the user asked

From `humanQuestions/2026-11-27.md`, answers 9, 17, and 19:
- the feeds themselves, their settings, their folders, and the reading and listening places sync;
- each device keeps its own downloaded episodes, whose files stay out of the sync for now.

Today a feed lives in `Feeds/<feed id>.json` of the data directory (`FeedRecord`, `FeedStore`), apart from library.json.
Its episodes are book rows with a `feedLink`, kept in the feed's own index (`FeedShelf`, `FeedIndex`).
The sync projects library.json alone (`SyncProjection.of`), so a feed's library folder crosses, and arrives with no feed to fill it.

## The phases

1. Subscriptions and each feed's own settings sync, so a feed's folder arrives with its feed (built on `feature/feed-sync`).
2. Episodes' places, played marks, bookmarks, and deletions sync, keyed by the episode's identity.
3. Folders' listening options and the global feed settings, if the user wants them (questions 3 and 4).

## A feed as sync keys

A feed is one set of registers, `feed/<feed id>/<field>`, beside `book/` and `folder/` (`SyncKey.feed`, `SyncFeedField`).
Its ID is the subscription's (`Feed.id`), which names its record file on every device.

The fields sync only what a person decides, never what a refresh reads:
- `presence`: `true`, or null for Delete Feed…;
- `address`: `feedURL`, which a permanent redirect changes, the same way on every device that follows it;
- `subscribed`: `subscribedDate`, which never changes;
- `folder`: the library folder's ID (`Feed.folderID`), the same ID the folder's own keys use;
- `unsubscribed`: `unsubscribedDate`, or null while subscribed (Unsubscribe is a switch, FR11);
- `itemPolicy`: the feed's own "List and download" choice and number, or null for the global one;
- `deleteAfterPlayed`: the feed's own days, or null;
- `listening`: the feed's own listening options (FR19), or null for the standard ones.

Left out, each device reading them from the feed itself: the title, summary, site, author, `isSerial`, `entryTotal`, `newestItemDate`, `usesInsecureTransport`, the items, and the fetch state.
So a refresh writes no entry, which keeps the change files small and the devices from trading a title back and forth.
A device lacking the feed makes its record from `address`, `subscribed`, and `folder` (`Feed(id:feedURL:folderID:subscribedDate:)`), its title the address's host until its own refresh reads the feed.

## Applying a feed

`SyncMerge.apply` returns `SyncFeedApplication` beside the library's changes: feeds to add, feeds whose synced fields changed, and feeds to remove.
`LibraryViewModel.applySync` hands it to `FeedCenter.applySync(_:)`, which writes through `FeedStore.add`, `update`, and the Delete Feed path.
- A new feed waits, its keys unapplied, until its `address`, `subscribed`, and `folder` are all in the registers and its folder lands in a live folder (`SyncFolderPlan.landing`).
- A new feed that is subscribed is checked at once, so its folder lists its episodes; the newest-N rule then downloads what this device's own choice says.
- A feed's folder follows a fold: two same-named folders fold into the older, and a feed whose folder was the other one lists in the survivor.
- A feed removed elsewhere is deleted here as Delete Feed… deletes one, with this device's Trash setting, unasked; a feed with a download in use waits for a later sync, as a busy book does.

## The same feed subscribed on two devices

Two devices that subscribed one address on their own hold two feeds with two IDs.
- The address is compared as `FeedAddress.comparisonKey` compares it: host, path, and query, whatever the scheme or a trailing slash.
- A device never adds a feed whose address it subscribes already, as a book of a known `md5Hash` stays one (phase 1).
- Of two new feeds of one address, the older `subscribed` is added, then the lower ID, so every new device makes the same one.
- Two different feeds of one title, subscribed apart, have same-named folders, which `SyncFolderPlan` folds into one; a folder lists one feed (`FeedStore.feed(inFolder:)`), so phase 1 adds no feed into a folder another feed lists in, and that feed waits.
  Phase 1b: the plan leaves two feeds' folders unfolded and names the younger "News 2".
- Phase 1b: the device holding the younger copy records an alias (`feed/<younger>/presence` set to the older ID, as a folded folder's presence is), and its projection and apply then speak for the older ID, so settings changed on either device reach both.
  Re-IDing the local feed is refused: the ID names its record, its packages' folder, and every episode's `feedLink`.

## Episodes' places (phase 2)

An episode is `feedItem/<feed id>/<item>/<field>`, where `<item>` is the first 32 hex digits of the SHA-256 of `FeedItem.id`.
- `FeedItem.id` is the guid, else the enclosure address, else the link and title (`FeedItemIdentity.key`), worked out from the feed alone, so two devices find one key for one episode.
  The digest keeps a guid's slashes and length out of the key's text.
- Fields: `position` (as a book's, compared by chapter, offset, and date), `played` (`playedDate`), `deleted` (`deletedDate`, Delete Episode), and bookmarks as `feedBookmark/<feed id>/<item>/<bookmark id>`.
- `downloadDeletedDate` and the state stay each device's own: the files stay out.
- A place applies to the episode's row when the row exists (downloaded, or kept with its place after its download went, FR11 branch A2).
- Otherwise it stays in the registers, and the row the next download makes takes it before it opens (`SyncCenter.syncedEpisodeState(feedID:itemID:)`).
- An episode with a synced place and no download says it in its row's value: "Not downloaded, 12 minutes in" (question 5).
- An episode the record no longer keeps (the newest-N list let it go) keeps its keys; nothing is applied until the feed lists it again.

## Feed folders and library folders

A feed's folder is a library folder (`BookFolder`), so it keeps its three keys as now, and the feed points at it by ID.
- Phase 1 stops it arriving empty by sending its feed, which lists its episodes once checked.
- An older TextMouth (0.6.0) still makes it empty: it drops every `feed/` key (below).
- A folder's listening options (`BookFolder.listeningOptions`) and sort settings stay each device's own until phase 3.

## Conflicts and order

- Each key keeps its highest entry, as now: a later stamp wins a setting, so the last change of the feed's own choice, on either device, stands.
- No feed field is `byHand`: every one is a person's choice already.
- A removal stands only when it is later than every other entry of the feed (as a book's): a setting changed elsewhere after Delete Feed… brings the feed back.
- Unsubscribe and Resubscribe are one key, so the later switch wins.
- In a merge, folders apply first, then books, then feeds, so a new feed's folder is there when it lands.
- R7 holds for feeds: a feed missing at launch is put back, and only Delete Feed… in a running TextMouth removes one (`SyncLaunchHold` holds `feed/<id>/presence`).
- Until `FeedStore.hasLoaded`, the host gives no feeds (`SyncLibraryHost.syncFeeds` is nil): no feed key is recorded or applied, and the apply runs again once they load, so a launch never reads as every feed removed.

## Older and newer TextMouths

- `formatVersion` stays 1: an older TextMouth leaves out of `entries` an entry whose key it doesn't know (`SyncFormats.decodeChangeFile`), and `SyncKey(text:)` knows no `feed/`, so 0.6.0 reads every file and ignores feeds.
- A 0.6.0 device's snapshots hold no feed key; each newer device's snapshot holds them all, so nothing is lost.
- A field a later TextMouth adds to a value (a new listening option) must not be dropped by this one: `SyncValueCoding.comparable` reads a feed's settings through their types, so a value differing only in a field this version doesn't know is no change, and no stripped copy is recorded (V04).
- A feed field this version doesn't know is a key it doesn't know: left out, as above.
- No new key in library.json, config.json, bookInfo.json, or a feed's record (R5).

## Size and request cost

- A feed is 8 entries of about 150 bytes: 30 feeds add about 36 KB to a snapshot and nothing to a change file until a person changes a feed.
- Phase 2 adds an entry for each episode played or begun, about 200 bytes; a feed of 400 played episodes is 80 KB, and Mark All Played writes them in one change file.
- No new file kind and no new upload: the entries ride in the change files and snapshots that already go.
- Each device fetches each feed itself, so subscribing on the Mac costs the iPhone one feed request per feed and the downloads its own choice asks for.

## Questions for the user

1. A feed deleted on one device is deleted on the other, its downloads there going to the Trash as Delete Feed… sends them? Recommended: yes (built).
2. The feed's own settings ("List and download", days after playing, listening options) sync, so a feed set to download its newest 3 on the Mac does so on the iPhone too? Recommended: yes (built); the iPhone's cellular switch still holds downloads.
3. The global feed settings (refresh interval, at launch, the global "List and download", alerts) stay each device's own? Recommended: yes, since a phone and a Mac want different checking.
4. A folder's listening options sync with the folder? Recommended: yes, in phase 3, with the folders' sort settings.
5. An episode begun on another device and not downloaded here says "Not downloaded, 12 minutes in"? Recommended: yes.
6. The same feed subscribed on two devices before sync becomes one feed whose settings follow either device (phase 1b's alias)? Recommended: yes; phase 1 keeps each device's own copy, one feed on each.

## What phase 1 builds

- `SyncFeedField`, `SyncKey.feed`, and `SyncFeedValues`, in `TextMouth/Models/Sync/`.
- `SyncProjection.of(_:contents:feeds:)` and `SyncMerge.apply(_:to:feeds:voices:)`, with the feeds nil while not loaded.
- `SyncLaunchHold` holding feeds; the comparable form of feed settings.
- The host's half: `SyncLibraryHost.syncFeeds`, `FeedCenter.applySync(_:)`, and recording when a feed's record changes.
- Unit tests on the projection, the merge, the duplicates, the folds, the launch, and an older TextMouth's reading.
