## Description

The iPhone has no export: no Export dialog and no Convert To (exportraw's finding, November 17, 2026). The Mac writes Plain Text, Markdown, HTML, RTF, Word 97-2003, EPUB, BRF, and PEF. Bring them to the iPhone, braille's BRF and PEF included, which DR34's parity list names (braille translates in the app since 1820f9853), with a destination the iPhone allows, such as the share sheet or Files. The user's aim (November 16): the iPhone at or near parity with the Mac.
