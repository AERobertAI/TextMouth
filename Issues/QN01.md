## Description

liblouis, LAME, and FFmpeg are LGPL 2.1 and linked statically on the iPhone. That is fine for TextMouth's public source, since anyone can relink (section 6a), but before any binary leaves the project (TestFlight, the App Store, or a release download), section 6's source offer and the App Store's terms against section 10 must be settled, a question for the user then. phonebraille and its review also recommend an in-app notice that names the three libraries, beside the Licenses folder, in the iPhone's About, since the folder alone may not be prominent. FU-FFMPEG2 holds the same question for FFmpeg.
