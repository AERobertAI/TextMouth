## Description

**DR36 — Read Word documents older than Word 6 on the iPhone.** From the ChatGPT docs review of November 16, 2026 (suggested priority P3; list item 30). Docs: `docs/user/formats-list.md`, "Office documents": "Word document older than Word 6 that is a compound file: read on the Mac by Apple's textutil, with headings guessed from font sizes; iPhone: no; Basic." and "Word 1 and Word 2 document (.doc): refused at once; iPhone: no." Code: Word 6 through Word 2003 are read by TextMouth's own `WordDocumentReader.swift` on both platforms; the older compound files go through `Services/TextutilConverter.swift`. **What keeps the iPhone from it today:** textutil is a separate program, and iOS lets an app start none, so a textutil format has no path there. Closing it needs a reader of TextMouth's own for the pre-Word-6 compound layout, which has no documented format, for files that are rare. Word 1 and Word 2 are refused on both platforms, so only the compound pre-Word-6 files are a parity gap. Platform: iPhone (a parity gap, small). Related: DR34. (question only until now: `humanQuestions/2026-11-03.md` question 9, recommended "no .doc or .dot import and no Word 97-2003 export on iOS at first; RTF and RTFD from the start; .odt and Word 2003 XML later if you use them". Since then TextMouth's own readers for Word 97-2003, RTF, ODT and Word 2003 XML landed on both platforms (0.3.1 and the November 5 to 8 campaign), so only the oldest files remain.)
