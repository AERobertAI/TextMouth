## Description

The user's answers of Nov 27 (humanQuestions/2026-11-27.md, 3, 5): Play has no actions and no long press: swipe up and down rewind and fast forward, and nothing else. While a timer runs, the Sleep Timer control is fully functional: swipe up and down to restart it or cancel it, double tap for the Sleep Timer menu. Like Audible, shaking the phone within about a minute after a timer ends restarts it.
