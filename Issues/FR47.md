## Description

TextMouth reads .docx and Word 97-2003 with its own readers on both apps (DOCXParser, and the November 5 Word reader), but writes Word only on the Mac, through AppKit's .docFormat (WordDocExportWriter), which Apple provides on the Mac alone; nothing writes .docx. The user chose on November 26, 2026 to write both with TextMouth's own code, for 0.7.0, the formats release: a .docx writer (WordprocessingML in a ZIP, with headings at their outline levels, which the Mac's .doc lacks; about 600 lines in the export design's estimate) and a Word 97-2003 writer (a compound file of its own tables), shared by both apps, the Mac's AppKit writer then retired. FR31's phase 4 (research/IPHONE-EXPORT-DESIGN-2026-11-24.md, question 4).
