## Description

With sync (FR43), a book not downloaded on a device is a placeholder: its record and a pointer to its files in the TextMouth Sync folder, without its text. Search Library can't search inside a placeholder.
The user wrote on November 25, 2026: "I feel like a library search is rare enough it should be fine if one just downloaded all the text or required all the text to be downloaded if the library is in the cloud before letting you do one, would it be faster to download one at a time to check it one by one or to trigger all of the downloads? put that on the queue to investigate..."

The user's library, counted read-only on November 25: 772 content.md files, 726.7 MB (median 660 KB, 90th percentile 2.0 MB, 99th 3.9 MB, 6 over 5 MB, the largest 30.1 MB); 318.3 MB with gzip -1; the whole library 12 GB.

To investigate:
- How long fetching every book's text takes from iCloud Drive, one book at a time against every download started at once (case 6 of the spike's `SyncRealDriveTests`, on made-up files with that distribution), on the Mac, and on the iPhone once the app is on it.
- Search Library on a device with placeholders: offer to fetch every book's text first, with its size, an estimate of the time, progress, and Cancel, all spoken; or search the text already there and offer the rest; or keep a compressed text bundle in the sync folder (one file of about 320 MB rather than 772), and what keeping it current would cost.
- Whether text, once fetched, stays on the device, and how it counts against the unopened-days rule (FR43, section 16).
