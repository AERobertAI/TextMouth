## Description

From review-l10npilot (Nov 18, 2026; build/orchestrator/nov18/review-l10npilot/report.md). 1. Pass -testLanguage en -testRegion US to every xcodebuild test (local runs, trains, GitHub) and launch the iPhone interface tests with -AppleLanguages (en) by default, so the suite never depends on the test Mac's language or region once a language ships (times follow the region: ReaderShownStateTests expects 4:59, which Finland writes 4.59). 2. l10n.py apply accepts plural forms for any key with an integer placeholder even when the English has none (Russian, Polish and Arabic need them for '%lld selected books'; translate.py refuses them today). 3. Arabic and Hebrew: the zero and two forms may leave out the number where CLDR says they are exact, as one may (translate.py); warn when an Arabic translation puts an English title without bidi isolation. 4. l10n.py shared-keys: the SwiftUI literals elsewhere whose text is a catalog key, and every string comparison of a catalog key (MenuVisibilityManager compared menu titles; the reviewer's scratch scanner is build/orchestrator/nov18/review-l10npilot/shared_keys.py). 5. After question 15 of humanQuestions/2026-11-18.md: a build step that ships each language's Localizable.strings as compact text and its .stringsdict as binary (measured on the pilot's Spanish: 22,576 bytes to 14,794), before signing, with a test that every lookup is unchanged.
