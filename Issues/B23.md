## Description

**What happens.** French puts a no-break space before `;`, but two fragments the app joins after a word begin with the semicolon itself, `"; %@ restent"` and `"; %@ reste"` (`FeedCommands.swift`, lines 855 and 857), and `apply` refuses leading whitespace that differs from the English's, so French shows "…; …" with no space before the semicolon. The Unsubscribe announcements that add a clause straight after a word do the same.

**The fix.** Let the code join the clause, with the language's own separator (a translatable "%1$@; %2$@" whose French puts the no-break space), not a fragment that starts with the punctuation. Found by `l10nfr3` and `l10nfrconsistency` (FR36).
