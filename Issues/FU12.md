## Description

make typecheck-tests with Xcode 27.2 (Nov 27) lists 13 warnings in the app. Eleven are ImplicitStrongCapture ('weak' ownership of capture 'self' differs from implicitly-captured strong reference in outer scope): BookManifestEditor.swift:209 (self and library), FeedCenter.swift:482, 491, 499, LibraryViewModel+ManifestEdits.swift:161, +MetadataRefresh.swift:139, +Migration.swift:2305, +PlaceSnapshots.swift:84, +TextAnchors.swift:131, and FeedCommandRunner.swift:385. Two are DeprecatedDeclaration in RecordingCaptureBackend.swift:355 and 373 (withCheckedThrowingContinuation(isolation:), replaced by the nonisolated(nonsending) overload). Each capture may be a real retain or a false alarm, so each needs a look; fixes must still build with Xcode 26.0.1 on GitHub.
