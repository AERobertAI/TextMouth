## Description

The user asked on November 25, 2026 for downloaded books to be removed from a device after a number of days unopened, 1 to 30 with 7 the default, kept books excepted, and for the device to download a kept book again after a sync. FR43 (0.6.0) keeps a book's files only where it is downloaded or kept, but removes nothing a device already has. The design is in research/CLOUD-SYNC-BUILD-PLAN-2026-11-25.md section 16, 'Books unopened for some days': a setting of each device, Off or 1 to 30 days, 7 by default on the iPhone and off on the Mac until the user turns it on (question 13 of humanQuestions/2026-11-25.md); a book removed only once its files are in the TextMouth Sync folder (the read-back that proves the copy); a kept book or one on the reading list never removed; every removal spoken. Needs the read-back of FR46 first.
