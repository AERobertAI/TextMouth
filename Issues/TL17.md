## Description

In train 1120-0144 the iPhone report said 1 failure: LibraryMoveTransactionTests.testFolderRenamePersistsNewBookInfoLocation "started and never finished". xcodebuild's own verdict was TEST EXECUTE SUCCEEDED, and its suite line says 12 tests, 0 failures: the app's background line "Failed to generate content cache for Movable Book: ..." was printed into the middle of the test's result line, so the reader never saw "passed". The reader should take a test as finished when its suite's own totals say so, or read result lines that another line interrupts, and the train should not call such a run FAILED.
