## Description

**FR23 — Speech and playback settings with a feed, folder, or Reading List scope.** Requested November 15, 2026: "allow a nother level of scope for speech settings like speed (is pitch supported in the app? that might be another thing) but for feeds to have a global settings for audio or text, a feed setting, and a local setting. maybe have one for folders and the reading list too when opening a book in it, but you have to open the folder's / feeds settings sheet to set that you want the scope... try having a agent plan this out." Today a book may keep its own voice, speed, and pitch (`Book.hasOwnSettings`, `BookSettingsWording`) over the global ones, and feeds and folders have Feed Settings and Folder Settings sheets for their listening options (FR19). Wanted: a middle scope, chosen in the feed's or folder's settings sheet (and the Reading List's), for text speech (voice, speed, pitch) and audio playback (speed) apart, resolving book, then that scope, then global. Planning first (agent `scopeplan`).
