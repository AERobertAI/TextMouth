## Description

From review-ffmpegform (November 17, 2026), item 5. The FFmpeg session skips any run of packets the decoder refuses, as it did before FU-FFMPEG2, and the gap closes: every later chapter mark lands late by the gap's length. A longer gap fails the import's length check (1 second plus 0.5%, ImportAudioConverter.swift about line 1033), so a book with a damaged stretch is refused with 'it lasts 1.0 seconds, not 2.8'. The sound around the damage is kept by the helper, but not by the import. Fix: fill a refused stretch with silence by its timestamps, so the length and the chapter marks hold; or tell the listener how much was skipped. A file with no good packet ends as damaged (exit 6), which is the truer sentence.
