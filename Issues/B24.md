## Description

Found by the French, Brazilian Portuguese, and German reviewers: SpeechService.pitchLabel formats with a fixed point, so French and German show 1.2x beside Normal (1,0x); TimeEntry and Go to Percent accept only a point, so a person who types 2,5 is refused. Format with the locale, and accept the locale's decimal mark.
