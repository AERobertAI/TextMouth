## Description

FR40 added `require_braille_pack` to `Scripts/check.sh`: a Release app must hold exactly one BrailleTables.pack in its resources, within its limit, and no raw liblouis table.
The self-test's stand-in build writes a Strings.pack into its made-up app but no BrailleTables.pack, so 35 cases of `Scripts/check-selftest.sh` fail on main after the FR40 merge (64 passed, 35 failed), as on the FR40 branch alone (63 passed, 35 failed).
Nothing ran the self-test in FR40's fix rounds.

Fix: the stand-in app holds a small BrailleTables.pack, and new cases cover `require_braille_pack`: no pack, a second one, one outside the resources folder, one over its limit, and a raw table file.
