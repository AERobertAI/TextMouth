## Description

fixtaps (November 26) traced UIKit taking 68 to 95 ms over the start of a touch on the Export sheet's switches, on the GitHub runner, so iOS 26's switch dropped XCUITest's 66 ms tap; the test now holds a switch a quarter of a second (d28f1295c). A person's finger or VoiceOver's double tap isn't affected the same way, but a main thread busy that long at a touch is worth finding: profile the reader and the sheet with Instruments' Time Profiler on a phone, and look for launch work still on the main thread (the Library's redraws during the audit, the protection cache's stat calls on rows, the storage rows).
