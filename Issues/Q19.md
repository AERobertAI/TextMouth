## Description

The Export sheet's interface tests tap a kind switch at 0.88 of its row and wait for the row it brings: Convert to after Text as Markdown, Format after Text as spoken audio. The tap sometimes doesn't take: the sheet stays open, the switch enabled, its value 0. Two causes were found and fixed on November 26: a view observing the whole reader (the iPhone sync gate, b9ea61781; the password overlay, a626fb492) and a second .alert on the Library root (3b42203a2). But 'No Convert to row' also failed in trains 1124-2142, 1125-0957, 1125-1123, and 1125-1421, before either, so the test may be flaky of itself. Hunt it: run ExportUITests many times on main (a train repeating the class), and if it fails, find why the tap is lost (timing of the tap after the sheet's list appears, an animation, the row's hit area) rather than retrying it. Per the standing rule, a pass on retry is a bug to hunt.
