# Podcasts and article feeds

Part of [TextMouth's documentation](../../README.md).

TextMouth subscribes to podcasts and article feeds, and lists each feed's episodes and articles in a library folder of its own, while it keeps their downloads in a folder of the feed's own in the data directory, with an index of their own, so the library's index lists books only (What TextMouth reads, above, gives the formats, the refreshes, and the downloads). A feed's commands, and an episode's, are in their rows' context menus and among the rows' actions in VoiceOver's Actions menu (VO-Command-Space), under the same names.

- Subscribing:
  - Library menu, Add Feed… (Shift-Command-F), or the Add Feed… button of the toolbar's Add to Library sheet (see [Adding books](adding-books.md)), opens the Add Feed sheet. Type or paste the feed's address, or the address of a web page that names its feed, and choose where the feed's folder goes with the Move dialog's folder picker, whose New Folder makes one; it offers every folder but a feed's own, starting at the folder the window shows. Return presses Subscribe, and Escape presses Cancel. While the picker's New Folder fields are shown, Return presses their Create instead, and nothing until a name is typed; once they close, Return presses Subscribe again, as in the Move dialog. Escape closes the New Folder fields first, and presses Cancel only once they are closed. In the Search field, Return searches, and never makes a folder, even with the fields shown.
  - Below the address, Search for a podcast searches Apple's podcast directory for the words you type, from a podcast's title or its publisher's name, when you choose Search, or press Return, which in that field searches instead of subscribing; only those words go to Apple. Up to 50 podcasts are listed, each with its publisher and number of episodes, which VoiceOver reads as one line, beside a Subscribe button of its own, which puts its feed's address in the address field and subscribes to it. VoiceOver hears how many were found, as in "3 podcasts found for “braille”."; a search that finds nothing, or fails, as when the directory refuses more than about 20 searches a minute, says so in the sheet and to VoiceOver.
  - TextMouth reads the feed first, then makes a folder named after it, numbered when the name is taken, so a failure leaves the library as it was: the failure stays in the sheet with its reason, which VoiceOver hears. A page naming several feeds lists them, to choose one. A feed, and an OPML file, that has a tag of more than 1,024 attributes, or is written in an encoding TextMouth can't read safely (UTF-7, say, or an ISO-2022 or EBCDIC one), fails with the reason that any damaged feed gives, as in "The feed is damaged at line 1: it has a tag with too many attributes for TextMouth to read safely." or "The feed is damaged at line 1: it is written in the UTF-7 encoding, which TextMouth cannot read safely.", and an OPML file with "That OPML file is damaged at line 1: " and the same reason. The check is made before the feed is read, so a feed built to stall the reader costs nothing. A feed that defines entities of its own, as some old feeds do, is still read. A moment after the sheet closes, VoiceOver hears what came, as in "Subscribed to Planet Money: 381 episodes.", or, under Only the newest 10 listed, "Subscribed to Planet Money: 10 of 381 episodes listed." (see Feeds in the library, below), or, for a feed that uses plain http, an alert warns once, saying the subscription was made.
  - Library menu, Import OPML… subscribes to each feed in another feed reader's export, each into a folder of its own inside the folder you pick, taking the first feed of a web page that names several, and then says which were subscribed already, which failed and why, and which use plain http; a feed it lists that is in your library, unsubscribed, stays unsubscribed and is named, as in "Finish It! is in your library, unsubscribed. Choose Resubscribe on its row, or in its Feed Info, to check it again." Cancel stops it after the feed under way. Return presses Import, and Escape presses Cancel; while the picker's New Folder fields are shown, Return presses their Create instead, and Escape closes them before it presses Cancel, and once the import has ended Return presses Done. Export OPML… saves every feed you subscribe to as OPML 2.0, leaving out those you unsubscribed from and saying how many, as in "Exported 3 subscriptions to TextMouth Subscriptions.opml. 1 feed you unsubscribed from was left out.", or says there is none to export.
- Feeds in the library:
  - Where there are feeds, a Feeds section follows the folders: at the top of the library and in All Items it lists every feed, in a folder the feeds whose folders are inside it, and in Unopened a feed only while it has a new episode or article among those it lists, until its last new one is played, opened, marked played, deleted with Delete Episode… (Delete Article…), or no longer listed; Recently Opened and the Reading List list none. Each row says, in one sentence, the feed's title, Feed, its episodes counted, and its last check, or why it failed, as in "Planet Money, Feed, 381 episodes, 5 downloaded, 1 downloading, 2 new, 370 unplayed, checked today at 3:05 PM". Opening the row opens the feed's folder; when that folder is gone, VoiceOver says so and Feed Info opens, where you can choose another. A feed you unsubscribed from says so after Feed, and since when it hasn't been checked, as in "Finish It!, Feed, unsubscribed, 10 of 381 episodes listed, 2 downloaded, not checked since October 9 at 3:05 PM"; its row and its folder's row show an Unsubscribed badge, and entering its folder says "unsubscribed" after Feed too.
  - The Feeds view lists every feed, subscribed or not, each by title and each row as the Feeds section says it, and nothing else. Choose it in the Library window's View pop-up (see [The Library window](library-window.md)). As shipped it is offered When Applicable, so the pop-up offers it only while there is a feed, and it comes last in the pop-up. Opening a feed's row there opens the feed's folder, in Folders. The Library tab of Settings can move it, offer it always, or hide it. A view of your own can list feeds too: one set to list Feeds with the condition New Items, "Has New Episodes or Articles", lists the feeds with a new episode or article, as Unopened lists them beside the books.
  - A feed's episodes are counted the same way wherever TextMouth counts them: on its row, on its folder's summary bar, entering its folder, in VoiceOver's Folders rotor, in Feed Info, and in Speak Info. The count begins with how many of the feed's entries TextMouth lists, of how many the feed lists, as in "10 of 381 episodes listed": under the first two List and download choices, every entry you haven't deleted; under Only the newest 10 listed, the newest 10, less those played whose download isn't here, beside every one downloaded, downloading, or waiting to download, wherever it falls (see Listing and downloading, below). Then come the episodes downloaded, downloading, and new, and last those not yet played, each left out when it is 0; one waiting to download counts as neither downloaded nor downloading. When TextMouth lists every entry the feed lists, the count is the number alone, as in "381 episodes"; a feed longer than the 2,000 entries TextMouth reads says "more than 2000", as in "10 of more than 2000 episodes listed"; and articles count "unread" rather than "unplayed". The feed's size counts the episodes you deleted too, since the feed still lists them. A feed subscribed before October 7, 2026 gives its size after its next check, which reads the whole feed once.
  - Each feed is listed once. In Folders, a feed's folder has no row of its own, since the feed's row in the Feeds section stands for it: the row opens the folder, and its context menu ends with the folder's Rename Folder… and Move Folder…. A feed's folder holding only its items offers no Delete Folder…: Delete Feed… (see Commands, below) deletes the feed, and its folder when nothing else is in it, so a feed has one command that deletes it. A feed's folder that also holds books or folders of your own does offer Delete Folder…; its first question ends "It is the folder of the feed “Finish It!”: whichever you choose, the feed stays, with its downloads, but no folder lists them until Feed Info gives the feed another folder; Delete Feed… on the feed's row deletes a feed.", and its second names the feed as staying without a folder. Delete Folder… on a folder holding feeds' folders, at any depth, asks first and never deletes a feed: its first question ends, as in "It holds the folder of the feed “Planet Money”. With Move Contents Out, the feed's folder moves with the rest. With Move Everything to Trash, the feed stays, with its downloads, but no folder lists them until Feed Info gives the feed another folder; Delete Feed… on the feed's row deletes a feed.", and its second, "The feed “Planet Money” stays, with its downloads, without a folder until Feed Info gives it another." VoiceOver's Folders rotor still lists the feed, as in "Planet Money, Feed, 381 episodes, 5 downloaded", and moves to its row. Entering the folder says its name, Feed, and its episodes counted. It lists the episodes newest first, under a heading that counts them, as in "Episodes (12)", or "Episodes (10 of 381 listed)" when the feed lists more than TextMouth does, and "Episodes (2)" while a search narrows them: a downloaded one as its book's row, and any other with, in this order, Played (Read for an article); its download's state, with why a download failed or waits, as in "Waiting to Download, it downloads once this Mac is on a network without data limits" or "Waiting to Download, it needs room for 61 MB on this Mac"; for one whose download went after you opened it, the place kept for it as its book's row said it, as in "42 percent, 16 minutes remaining", or "Finished" alone for one kept at its end (none for a played one), and its bookmarks, as in "2 bookmarks"; New; its date; and its duration. The screen shows the same details, in shorter forms such as "42%" and "16:00 left", and a row that says a place has the hint "Downloads it again, then opens it where you left off". The folder's other books follow, under Books. The summary bar gives the feed's count and its last check, as its row does, beside the listening time left in the folder's books.
  - An episode is New when it was published after you subscribed, and has been neither played nor opened.
  - At the first launch of the October 10, 2026 version, an episode or article downloaded earlier, which the library listed as a book in the feed's folder, moves into the feed's own folder in the data directory, keeping its place, bookmarks, settings, place in the reading list, and history in Stats; a download of a feed whose record is gone, as one an earlier version unsubscribed you from, stays a book of the library, where it was. An older version of TextMouth doesn't see the downloaded episodes and articles, which its library's index no longer holds, and marks them not downloaded; this version marks them downloaded again at its next launch, each whose file is on this Mac, while one whose file is only in the cloud stays not downloaded until a launch finds its file here.
- Listing and downloading:
  - Every feed follows one List and download choice, the one for every feed, set in the Feeds tab of Settings, or one of its own, set in its Feed Info (below): Every item listed, newest 5 downloaded, the default; Every item listed, downloads by hand; or Only the newest 5 listed, where 5 stands for the number that goes with the choice, from 1 to 2,000, 5 unless you change it. A feed's list follows a change of the choice or the number at once; what downloads, and what is removed, follows once neither has changed for ten seconds.
  - Under the default, a feed's folder lists every episode and article the feed lists, and TextMouth keeps downloaded those of the newest 5 not yet played, newest by publication date, a serial show's too: it downloads each of them by itself, at launch, after you subscribe, after each refresh, and once one of its own downloads finishes, an episode only on a network without data limits. Played ones count among the newest 5, so playing one never downloads an older one, while deleting one with Delete Episode… brings the next older one in. When TextMouth holds more than 5 of its own downloads of a feed, as once a newer episode has arrived, it deletes for good the files of its oldest downloads beyond the newest 5 until it holds 5, keeping, as Delete Download keeps them, the place, bookmarks, settings, and transcript of any you opened (see Commands, below). This rule never removes a download you asked for, one made before this version, one on the reading list, or one open in a reader, being transcribed, or being exported, or whose text is open in an editor with Edit Text, which it leaves for a later pass, nor, in a library in the cloud, one not wholly on this Mac, and a download that waits or fails removes nothing. Opening or playing one of TextMouth's own downloads doesn't make it yours: it still goes once it falls beyond the newest 5, its place kept. An item becomes yours when you ask for its download, even while TextMouth's own download of it waits or runs, when you choose Cancel Download or Delete Download on it (the Feed Downloads section's Cancel doesn't make it yours, below), or when a download you asked for fails: from then on the rule never downloads it by itself, nor removes its download, though the rule that deletes played downloads still does (see Commands, below). Nor does it download an item whose download was deleted before this version.
  - A download TextMouth makes by itself waits rather than failing: an episode on a network with data limits waits for the next refresh TextMouth makes by itself on a network without them, as does an article that must fetch its page because its feed gives only a short summary, and a download without room on this Mac for the size its feed gives, beside the 1 GB TextMouth keeps free, waits for a later pass that finds room. While this Mac is offline, such a download waits in the same way, for the next refresh TextMouth makes by itself that reaches the network, rather than failing; its row's one reason covers both: "it downloads once this Mac is on a network without data limits". A download refused on a network with data limits says the same, as in "“Ep. 368” downloads once this Mac is on a network without data limits." A download you ask for yourself fails when there is no room, and one TextMouth made by itself that failed is tried again only after a later refresh of its feed that reaches the network. A download TextMouth asked for by itself that hasn't started, waits, or failed is taken back, the item no longer downloaded, once the rule no longer wants it, as when the item is played or falls out of the newest: Mark Played, Mark All Played, and the end of a refresh ask for the rule's pass at once, which runs as soon as any sweep or pass already under way has finished, so no row keeps a failure nobody asked for. A download you asked for is never taken back.
  - Every item listed, downloads by hand, lists every episode and article the feed lists, and downloads nothing by itself. Only the newest 5 listed lists the newest 5, less those you have played or read that aren't downloaded, and every item downloaded, downloading, or waiting to, wherever it falls, and downloads nothing by itself either. Choosing either takes back the downloads TextMouth asked for and hadn't started, and removes none of those it made. Under Only the newest 5 listed, the feed's record keeps only the newest of that number, beside every item downloaded or downloading, played, or whose place is kept, so when you choose a longer list, the items it hid are listed again at once, and those it no longer holds come back at the feed's next refresh, which then reads the whole feed.
  - The first launch of this version moves the earlier settings over, once: Keep the newest 10 entries, as TextMouth shipped it, or Download new episodes and articles by themselves turned on, becomes the default; Keep every entry becomes Every item listed, downloads by hand; and any other number of entries becomes Only the newest of that number listed, at least 1. A feed's own number of entries, from its Feed Info, becomes its own Only the newest of that number listed, 10 included, and a feed's own Keep every entry makes it follow the choice for every feed when that lists every item, and otherwise gives it Every item listed, downloads by hand. So, in a library whose settings were as TextMouth shipped them, that launch downloads each feed's newest 5 not yet played or downloaded, its episodes on a network without data limits, and removes nothing.
- Downloads under way:
  - While episodes and articles download, each Library window shows a Feed Downloads section at its bottom, below the list, which it never covers. It shows only while downloads run: its heading and a Cancel button, a bar for the whole run, how many of the run's downloads are done, as in "3 of 9 done", with "1 failed" when one did, and the download under way, with its feed and how far its file has come, as in "Download 4 of 9: “Ep. 368” from Finish It!, 40% of 63 MB", then "adding it to the library" while its book is added. Once one has downloaded, it gives the time left, as in "About 4 minutes left", worked out by size where the feeds give sizes, and, for an article or an episode whose feed gives no size, by the time such downloads took, as the import overlay works out its own. Downloads TextMouth asked for by itself that wait are counted apart, by their reason, as in "2 waiting until this Mac is on a network without data limits" or "1 waiting for room on this Mac". How far a file has come, and the time left, are shown anew every 5 seconds.
  - VoiceOver reads every line as the section's label, as in "Feed Downloads: 3 of 9 done. Download 4 of 9: “Ep. 368” from Finish It!, 40% of 63 MB. About 4 minutes left.", and calls its Cancel button Cancel Feed Downloads. For a run of 10 seconds or more, VoiceOver also hears, wherever you are, each quarter of the run's downloads, as in "Feed downloads: 3 of 12 done, about 4 minutes left.", and how the run ended, as in "Feed downloads finished: 8 downloaded, 1 failed.", or, for a run of one download, "Finished downloading “Ep. 368”." A run TextMouth began by itself, which nobody heard start, is announced once, after ten seconds, with how many downloads are under way and the time left, as in "Feed downloads: 9 under way, about 4 minutes left.", unless a quarter has been heard or Cancel pressed by then.
  - Cancel stops every feed download, whether you or TextMouth asked for it: the one under way, those waiting to start, and those TextMouth asked for that wait for a network or for room; and it deletes what they had downloaded, partial files included. A download whose book is already being added to the library finishes, as in "“Ep. 368” is already being added to the library."; meanwhile the section says "Cancelling..." in place of the time left, and its Cancel is dimmed, until the run ends or a download you ask for meanwhile begins, which gives Cancel back. Cancel is a pause: for a feed whose List and download choice downloads by itself, TextMouth downloads its newest again by itself once a check of the feed that began after Cancel has ended, the regular check, Check Feed, or Refresh All Feeds, or the next time TextMouth opens; Download on an item still downloads it at once, and Cancel Download on an item's row is still how you keep TextMouth from downloading that item by itself. A moment after Cancel, VoiceOver's focus moves to the Library window's list, since the section goes once the run ends, and only then does VoiceOver say what Cancel did, as in "Stopped 6 feed downloads. TextMouth downloads each feed's newest again at its next check, or the next time it opens." VoiceOver says how what Cancel stopped comes back: that line when the feeds will download all of it by themselves; "To download it again, choose Download on its row." (or "them", "their rows") when none would come back by itself, as an older episode you asked for, or a feed that downloads by hand or that you unsubscribed from; and for a mix, as in "TextMouth downloads 1 of them again at its feed's next check, or the next time it opens. To download the other one again, choose Download on its row." The button's hint begins "Stops every feed download, those waiting for a network or for room too, and deletes what they had downloaded." and then says the same of how they come back before you press it.
  - Library menu, Speak Download Progress has VoiceOver speak the section's lines on request, or say "No feed download is under way.", followed by the downloads waiting, if any, as in "No feed download is under way. 2 waiting until this Mac is on a network without data limits." Give it a key in the Shortcuts tab of Settings, under Information, to use it in every window but an audio editor's (see Works in, under the Shortcuts tab).
  - An episode's or article's row says "Downloading" while its download waits to start or runs, without a percent, since the section says how far it has got; a place kept for it is read beside it as "42 percent read".
- Commands:
  - For a feed, on its row: Check Feed, which says what came; Feed Info…, which Command-I opens too, as does the Feed Settings button in the Library window's toolbar, in the feed's folder (see [The Library window](library-window.md)); Mark All Played (Mark All Read for articles); Unsubscribe, or, for a feed you unsubscribed from, Resubscribe in place of Check Feed and Unsubscribe; and, last, Delete Feed… (below). When unsubscribing would delete what the feed kept of downloads you removed, the command is Unsubscribe…, with an ellipsis, and asks first, saying what happens, as in "TextMouth stops checking this feed for new episodes. The feed stays in your library with its settings and its 2 downloaded episodes, and Resubscribe checks it again. It also deletes the place and bookmarks kept for 1 removed download."; Unsubscribe without an ellipsis acts at once. Unsubscribing only stops checking: the feed keeps its folder, its list, its settings, and its downloads, and VoiceOver says so, once any question has closed, as in "Unsubscribed from Finish It!: TextMouth no longer checks it for new episodes; its 2 downloaded episodes stay. The place and bookmarks kept for 1 removed download were deleted.", or, for a feed with one download, "its downloaded episode stays"; what it kept of downloads you removed is deleted, to the Trash or for good as Removal Behavior in Storage settings says, except for an item downloading again, and the downloads TextMouth had asked for by itself and not yet started are taken back, while the one under way, and any you asked for, go on. Unsubscribe deletes only the kept places its question named; when it asks nothing it deletes nothing, and a kept place whose waiting download it takes back stays kept. While you are unsubscribed, TextMouth never checks the feed; its rules still delete played downloads and those beyond its newest, but download nothing by themselves, and Download still works when you ask. Resubscribe checks the feed again from then on, and at once, on any network, as in "Resubscribed to Finish It!. Checking it now…", then says what came; under a List and download choice that downloads by itself, the newest items it lacks then download, on a network without data limits. Add Feed… with an unsubscribed feed's address says "Finish It! is in your library, unsubscribed. Choose Resubscribe on its row, or in its Feed Info, to check it again." A launch that finds a feed's folder in the data directory with no record of the feed, as after a lost record or an Unsubscribe made by an earlier version, returns its downloads as books at the top of the library, without an announcement, keeping their place, playback times, bookmarks, settings, transcript, place on the reading list, and history in Stats, and deletes what the feed kept of removed downloads and then, once nothing else is in it, the feed's own folder: at once in a library outside the cloud, and in a library in the cloud only once this Mac has found the record missing at two of its own launches, since a feed you subscribe to on another Mac can reach this one before its record does. Another Mac's launches don't count, a record only in the cloud counts as there, and a launch that finds it again starts the count over; meanwhile no folder lists those downloads.
  - Delete Feed…, the last of a feed's commands on its row, among its VoiceOver actions, and in Feed Info, for a feed you subscribe to or one you unsubscribed from, is the one command that deletes a feed. It asks first, as in "Delete the feed “Finish It!”?", saying what goes, to the Trash or for good as Removal Behavior in Storage settings says: "The feed leaves your library, with its list of episodes and its settings. It deletes its 5 downloaded episodes and the place and bookmarks kept for 1 removed download: they go to the Trash, where you can recover them until the Trash is emptied. Its folder “Podcasts/Finish It!” goes too when nothing else is in it. To keep an episode, choose Move to Library… on its row first." Once the question has closed, VoiceOver says what went, as in "Deleted the feed “Finish It!”, its 5 downloaded episodes, and the place and bookmarks kept for 1 removed download. Its folder “Podcasts/Finish It!” is gone too.", or that the folder stays, as something else is in it. A kept episode downloading again as the feed is deleted goes as Removal Behavior says, with its place, bookmarks, and transcript, and if Delete Feed… stops short, no episode stays Downloading or Waiting to Download. The feed's downloads stop first, and what they had downloaded goes; then its downloads and what it kept of downloads you removed, which leave the reading list; then its own folder in the data directory; then its record, with its list and what you played, so subscribing to it again starts afresh; and last its library folder, when nothing else is in it, another feed's episodes included. Stats keep their history, and an episode or article you moved into the library stays a book of yours. While one of the feed's downloads is open in a reader, being transcribed, or being exported, Delete Feed… deletes nothing and says why in an alert, which VoiceOver reads; it is not announced besides, as in "“Ep. 368” is open in a reader. Close it, then choose Delete Feed… again."; a download that can't be deleted keeps the feed, with whatever wasn't deleted, the alert naming each and why.
  - For an episode or article: Open, which downloads it first; Download, or Cancel Download while it downloads, and both for one waiting to download by itself, where Download downloads it at once, on any network, and Cancel Download leaves it to you, saying, as in "“Ep. 368” no longer waits to download; it downloads when you choose Download."; Mark Played or Mark Unplayed (Mark Read or Mark Unread for an article); Show Description…; for a downloaded one, Show in Finder and Delete Download; and Delete Episode… (Delete Article…), which asks first, deletes everything kept for it, its download included, and keeps it out of the list for good; when TextMouth keeps anything of yours for the item, such as its place, the question adds "The place, bookmarks, and transcript kept for it are deleted too.", and once the question has closed VoiceOver says, as in "Deleted “Ep. 368”." Delete Download, the library's Remove of episodes and articles, Delete Episode…, and Delete Feed… refuse a download that is open in a reader, being transcribed, or being exported, so no transcript or export under way is lost, and say why and what to do, as in "“Ep. 368” is being transcribed, and deleting its download now would lose the transcript. Wait until the transcription ends, or cancel it, then try again."; TextMouth's own rules wait for such a download instead, and for an episode whose text is open in an editor with Edit Text, which your own deletions don't wait for. A downloaded episode's book row offers these beside the book's own commands, less Move to Folder… and Edit Audio…, which an episode or article never offers while it belongs to its feed, and Edit Text in Default Editor and Edit Text With…, which an article never offers and an episode offers once it is transcribed (see [Transcribing an audiobook](transcribing.md)).
  - Cancel Download chosen on an episode whose book is already being added to the library changes nothing, since the book arrives all the same, and VoiceOver says "“Ep. 368” is already being added to the library." instead of "Stopped downloading", the sentence the Feed Downloads section's Cancel says too; the episode stays as TextMouth's own rule asked for it, and is not made yours. Download or Open chosen right after Cancel Download downloads the episode, and opens it for Open, where the cancelled download's end used to swallow the new request. A download, or a check of a feed, that is cancelled just as it begins ends as cancelled, even while this Mac is offline, and no longer holds up every later download until TextMouth restarts.
  - An episode you deleted stays deleted when its feed lists less than it did. TextMouth keeps the record of a deleted episode that the feed no longer lists for 90 days after you deleted it, and when a read lists none of the feed's items, as a publisher's server sometimes sends, it keeps every deleted episode's record whatever its age, so none comes back as a new, unplayed episode that the newest-episodes rule downloads. New episodes a check found are not lost when TextMouth quits, or the library can't be saved, before the check's result is written: the next check reads the feed whole again and adds them.
  - Move to Library…, on a downloaded episode's or article's book row, among its VoiceOver actions, and in its reader's Book menu, makes it a book of the library's own; it has no key until you give it one in the Shortcuts tab of Settings, under Library, and then acts on the episodes and articles selected in a Library window, or on a reader's book. It opens the folder picker Move to Folder… uses, under a heading such as "Move “Ep. 368” to Library" and the line "Moves this episode into a folder of your library, where it becomes a book you can edit; it leaves the feed", which VoiceOver reads once, as text; the Move button gives no hint that says it again. It takes only an episode or article still downloaded in its feed. The book keeps its place, playback times, bookmarks, settings, transcript, place on the reading list, and history in Stats, where Books Finished still counts it as an episode, and VoiceOver says what happened a moment after the sheet has closed, as in "Moved “Ep. 368” into Podcasts/Keepers. It's a book of your library now, and its feed lists it In Library." A move that moves only some of the items keeps the sheet open and says both what moved and what stayed, and why, as in "Moved “Ep. 1” into Podcasts/Keepers. It's a book of your library now, and its feed lists it In Library. “Ep. 2” stays in its feed: The file couldn't be moved." Book Info, Edit Text, and Move to Folder… then work on it as on any book. The feed lists the item "In Library", as in "Ep. 368, In Library, July 17, 2026, 1 hour, 4 minutes", and its row's hint is "Opens its book in your library": Open opens the book; it counts among the newest and is never downloaded again by itself; Mark Played, Show Description…, and Delete Episode… stay, Delete Episode… asking "The episode leaves the list for good; the book it became in your library stays. Checking the feed won't bring it back."; and once the book leaves the library, the item becomes not downloaded. A book you move into the feed's own folder is listed there once, as its book's row, offering the episode's commands beside the book's. A move cut short never loses the book: the next launch finds it in its feed or in the folder chosen.
  - Deleting a download keeps your place. Delete Download removes the download's file, and an article's text, to the Trash or for good as Removal Behavior in Storage settings says, an article's file and its text going to the Trash together, as one item, deletes for good anything else made from the file, and keeps in the feed's own folder in the data directory the episode's or article's place, playback times, bookmarks, its own settings, any details you edited by hand, its transcript, and any copies Edit Text kept of an edited text; a download you never opened, holding none of these, goes entirely, to the Trash or for good as that setting says. VoiceOver then says what it kept, naming only what the download held, as in "Deleted the download of “Ep. 368”. Its place and bookmarks are kept for when you download it again.", or, when nothing was kept, "Deleted the download of “Ep. 368”. It can be downloaded again." The rule that deletes played downloads, and the one that removes TextMouth's own downloads beyond a feed's newest (see Listing and downloading, above), keep them the same way, though they delete the file for good, and they keep an article's text as well, since it may hold an edit you made before feed items lost Edit Text, while an article holding nothing of yours goes entirely, its text included. The item then shows as not downloaded, and leaves Recently Opened and the Reading List; after Delete Download, TextMouth never downloads it again by itself. Downloading it again brings back the same book, opening where you left off, and its transcript shows its text again when the new file holds the same audio; a host that inserts different advertisements in each download makes it stale until you transcribe the episode again. ## An episode's own transcript and chapters

- From version 0.4.2, a podcast episode brings the transcript and chapters its feed links, once its audio has downloaded. This is on for every feed. There is no switch yet.
- Transcript: TextMouth tries up to three of the episode's transcript links, in this order of format: WebVTT, Podcasting 2.0 JSON, then SubRip. A link to an HTML page or to plain text is never fetched, since it holds no times.
- Chapters: a Podcasting 2.0 chapters file becomes the episode's headings when its audio has none of its own. A chapter's title is cut to 200 characters.
- The episode then has text and headings at once. Its Book Information says "The podcast's own transcript, downloaded with the episode on …" (see [Transcribing an audiobook](transcribing.md)).
- Limits: a transcript may be 5 MB and a chapters file 1 MB. No link inside a file is followed or opened. The whole fetch stops after 2 minutes.
- A file that can't be fetched or read is left out. The episode's download never fails because of it, and the debug log names the episode and why.
- Downloading an episode again: its own transcript gives way to the newly fetched one, because a host that inserts different ads into each download makes the kept one stale. A transcript you made by recognition, edited, or cleaned up is always kept.
- Not yet: HTML and plain-text transcripts are not fetched, and a quit during the fetch downloads the episode again at the next launch.
