## Autoplay requests

Autoplay requests: Continue to Next Book.

- Owner: ReaderAutoplayRequests.shared, a ReaderRequestStore (Services/ReaderRequestStore.swift) of ReaderAutoplayRequest, the one shape the reading list's requests share (below): at most one request per book, keyed by the book, claimed at most once (consume(for:)), and thrown away once older than ReaderRequestStore.lifetime, 30 seconds, unless its book's reader holds its requests (hold(for:) and release(for:)), each told as it is made (requestsMade), and dropped by discard(for:). ReaderAutoplayRequests and ReadingListOpenRequests are the store's two kinds, each with its shared store and its request(…) in a constrained extension.
- Who may write it: a reader whose book ends with Continue to Next Book, or with a feed's or folder's choice to play the book below or above (EndOfBookRule, below), asks LibraryViewModel.succession(after:going:skippingFinished:) what follows, going the rule's way, and acts on the answer (goOnPastThisBook, below). For a plain folder's book the answer is nextBook(after:going:skippingFinished:), the first of booksBeyond(_:going:) that is wanted: the books beyond it in the folder's manual order as the list shows it (ManualBookOrder.orderedBooks), found by ListNeighbors.beyond(_:going:in:), passing over those that rest at their end (Book.restsAtEnd) when the rule skips finished books. For a feed's item it is the next item the feed's folder lists that way (FeedCenter.listedItems(of:), newest first, FR11, leaving out items moved into the library): a downloaded item's book, or, for an item whose file is not here, the order to download it (BookSuccession.episodeToDownload, below); when the rule skips finished books, an item whose book rests at its end is passed over, and so is one with no file here that was played (FeedItem.playedDate). The reader hands over to a book (handOverToNextBook(to:titled:askingWith:), which takes back the finished state, shows the book paused in Now Playing, and announces the next book); half a second later (continueToNextBookDelay), continueToNextBook(_:under:) leaves a request for it, and showNextBook shows the book as the window choice says (a book moves in place or in a new window, 0.2.5, in [Windows](windows.md)): by default this reader's own window switches to it, the new reader there claiming the request once loaded, and with A New Window, or whenever switching could lose work, it posts continueToNextBook, which LibraryWindowRouter turns into that book's reader, or brings it forward. With nothing to hand over to, the book says "No more books in folder" when no book lies that way and "Every book after it in the folder is finished" (EndOfBookWording.onlyFinishedBooksInFolderNote(going:), "before it" going up) when only finished books do. A book read from the reading list, and Next or Previous in Reading List from a playing book, leave a request the same way, through continueToNextBook(_:under:) (reading from the reading list, below).
- How others observe it: the next book's reader claims the request when it finishes loading, or at once when it was already open and loaded, and starts playing. A reader that cannot load its book discards the request. A reader that downloads its book from the cloud to open it holds its book's requests (FR02), from startDownload(.opening) until claimAutoplayRequest(), discardAutoplayRequest(), which a failed or cancelled download and a failed load call, or endCloudWorkForClose(), after which the request ages again from when it was made; so the next book starts once it has downloaded and loaded, however long that takes, and meanwhile a Play in the finished book's window still says "Still starting". A Library window that refuses an open while the library moves or the data directory is unavailable drops both kinds of request for the book (ReaderOpenRefusal.dropRequests(for:), from ContentView's two refusals).

## Library search requests
<!-- id: library-search-requests; keys: ; src: TextMouth/Services/LibrarySearchJumpRequests.swift, TextMouth/ViewModels/ReaderViewModel+LibrarySearch.swift, TextMouth/ViewModels/ReaderViewModel.swift, TextMouth/ViewModels/LibrarySearchModel.swift, TextMouth/Services/ReaderRequestStore.swift, TextMouth/Services/TextSearch/BookTextSearch.swift, Scripts/test/kf8-fuzz/Stubs.swift, TextMouth/Models/LibrarySearchWording.swift, TextMouth/Services/VoiceOverAnnouncer.swift, TextMouthProductionTests/LibrarySearchJumpTests.swift -->

Library search requests (libsearch, November 13, 2026): the move to a match that Search Library opened.

- Owner: LibrarySearchJumpRequests.shared, a ReaderRequestStore (Services/LibrarySearchJumpRequests.swift) of LibrarySearchJumpRequest, the same shape as the autoplay and reading list requests: at most one per book, claimed once, thrown away after 30 seconds. A request carries the book, the match's chapter, UTF-16 offset in it and in the book, the matched text, and the query and TextSearchOptions that found it. ReaderViewModel.libraryJumpRequests holds the store the reader reads (the shared one unless a test gives its own).
- Who may write it: LibrarySearchModel.open(_:), which makes the request and then posts .openBookWindow, so the router opens the book's reader window, or brings its open one forward, and refuses while the library moves; a book the library refuses to open drops its requests (ReaderOpenRefusal.dropRequests, with the reading list's and the autoplay's), so opening the book another way within 30 seconds never jumps to an old match.
- How others observe it: ReaderViewModel.claimLibrarySearchJump() (ReaderViewModel+LibrarySearch.swift) claims the request once the book has loaded, as its load ends for a new window, or at once for a reader open already (requestsMade); a closed reader claims nothing and a loading one leaves it waiting. A book with no text to search says so, as Search in Book does (announceNoTextToSearch). Otherwise LibrarySearchJumpPlace.atPlace(for:in:) checks the place on the main actor with one lookup, a literal query in a window one character either side, a regular expression by one attempt anchored at the place (BookTextSearch.Matcher.regexMatch(at:in:), under 0.1 ms at the last match of a 19 MB chapter, where a scan took 0.1 s), and moves at once when the place still holds the match. Only when the text changed since the search (Edit Text, a rebuild) does the reader search the whole book off the main actor (searchWholeBook, up to libraryJumpMatchLimit, 100,000 matches, cancellable: a later claim, closing, or another book stops it) and move to the match nearest the old place (LibrarySearchJumpPlace.nearest, the earlier of two equal), or say the match is gone. The move goes through navigatePreservingReturnPosition, as Search in Book's pick does, so Go Back returns to where the reader was (for a book that was not open, to its saved reading position), and honours Auto-play on navigate. What VoiceOver hears (LibrarySearchWording.movedToMatch(location:), movedToMatchFoundAgain, matchGone) is said through afterReaderComesForward, once the reader's window is in front, VoiceOverAnnouncer.afterSheetDelay (0.6 s) after the move, since an open reader claims the request inside the request, before the Search Library window brings it forward, and words said at once came while the window changed. They are not said once the reader has closed, has claimed a later request, or, for a move, once Go Back has undone it.
- Files: Services/LibrarySearchJumpRequests.swift, ViewModels/ReaderViewModel+LibrarySearch.swift, ViewModels/ReaderViewModel.swift, ViewModels/LibrarySearchModel.swift, Services/ReaderRequestStore.swift, Services/TextSearch/BookTextSearch.swift.
- Tests: LibrarySearchJumpTests, in the reader-text area of Scripts/test/areas.conf.

What a finished book is followed by (FR19): the rule that weighs the settings, and the wait for a feed's next episode.

- Owner: EndOfBookRule (Models/EndOfBookRule.swift), a value that ReaderViewModel.endOfBookRule resolves each time it is asked, from the book's own end behavior (Book.localEndOfBookBehavior; a raw value that names no EndOfBookBehavior is ignored), the options of its feed or plain folder (LibraryViewModel.listeningOptions(of:), in [Feeds](feeds.md)), and the global setting (SettingsViewModel.endOfBookBehavior). It holds the behavior, the direction (ReadingList.Direction), skipsFinished, and predownloadsNext. EndOfBookRule.resolve is the one place the order is written: the book's own choice, which always goes down; else the options' choice (play the book below is Continue to Next Book going down, play the book above is Continue to Next Book going up, and stop is Stop); else the global setting, which goes down. The reading list, for a book opened from it, is asked first by the callers, and is no input. skipsFinished and predownloadsNext come from the options whichever layer decides, and are true when there are none. ReaderViewModel.effectiveEndOfBookBehavior is the rule's behavior alone, which the settings sheet's picker and the sleep timer's hints read, so the picker names Continue to Next Book for either direction, and its Use Global Default button falls back to the options' choice before the global setting. A reader that waits for an episode owns nextEpisodeWait, an AnyCancellable on the feed engine's events, and hasAskedForPredownload, with predownloadLeadTime (ListeningOptions.predownloadLeadTime, 10 minutes; a test lowers it).
- Who may write it: handleEndOfBook() and playFromTheEnd() ask the rule, and with the reading list's followReadingListPastThisBook(sleepTimerEndsListening:sleepTimerStoppedHere:sayingNothingFollows:) each ends in goOnPastThisBook(to:in:startingWith:sleepTimerEndsListening:sleepTimerStoppedHere:sayingNothingFollows:) (ReaderViewModel+GoingOn), the one copy of the steps the reading list's end and Continue to Next Book once each carried. It takes a BookSuccession: .book, which it hands over to unless this reader's sleep timer ends listening here (sleepTimerEndsListeningHere: it is fading out, or is set for the end of a heading or of the book, which this end reaches; then it says "Next book not started because of the sleep timer", and starts no download either; a timer counting minutes goes on with the next book, in [Timers and statistics](timers-and-statistics.md)); .nothing(note:), which it says why; or .episodeToDownload(feedID:item:), a feed's next episode whose file is not here. For that it says "Finished: <title>. Downloading the next episode: <next title>." (EndOfBookWording.downloadingNextNote; "article" for an article) and calls waitForNextEpisode(_:of:startingWith:) (ReaderViewModel+EpisodeDownload), which notes a NextEpisodeWait (the item, its feed, the closure that starts the book, controlsOwner, the reader that held the media controls as the wait began, NowPlayingSession.activeOwnerID, the NextEpisodeCause, .endOfBook or Next and Previous in Folder's .folderMove, and expiryMark, the sleep timer's expiry count as the wait began), listens to the feed engine's events first, then asks FeedCenter.download(_:of:startingNext:) with startingNext true, the listener's download, so the newest-N rule never removes it, one already waiting or running not started twice, and put ahead of the downloads queued before it, opening nothing since the wait hands over (in [Feeds](feeds.md)), and looks at the item's state: downloading or waiting, it waits; already downloaded, it goes on at once; anything else, the engine refused, and the reader says EndOfBookWording.nextEpisodeNotDownloaded(reason:isArticle:) with EndOfBookWording.downloadNotStartedReason. The item's downloadFinished event hands listening over as for any next book ("Starting: <next title>", goOnWithNextEpisode(as:during:)), unless the listener has moved on (listenerHasMovedOn(_:): the reader no longer rests finished, has closed, or plays, or another reader took the media controls since the wait began, by playing or by its window becoming the focused one; the controls returning to this reader, or staying with the reader that held them, is no move) or this reader's sleep timer ran out since the wait began, whether it was set before or during the wait, or is fading out (SleepTimerService.endedListening(ownedBy:sinceExpiry:), against expiryMark), when the episode is not started and nothing is said but what the timer said as it ran out; a running timer goes on with the episode, handOverToNextBook holding it for the book as for any next book. When the wait is for Next or Previous in Folder, a book that was playing hands over to the episode and any other only opens it, and the wait's failure and cancel use the folder commands' sentences (below). Its downloadFailed event says nextEpisodeNotDownloaded with the failure's own sentence, and its downloadCancelled event, which FeedDownloadManager sends when the listener cancels the download, says EndOfBookWording.nextEpisodeDownloadCancelled ("Download cancelled, so playback stopped.", one sentence for an episode and an article); each ends the wait, and neither is said, only traced (DiagnosticLog.trace), when the listener has moved on. The wait also ends when the reader closes (close() clears nextEpisodeWait), when another wait replaces it, and when a new end decision supersedes it: goOnPastThisBook clears it first, and playFromTheEnd() does as its first step, since its Stop, Starts Over, and Loop branches do not pass through goOnPastThisBook. predownloadNextEpisodeIfDue() (ReaderViewModel+Predownload) is called from publishLivePosition(now:) as the position publishes, cheapest guard first, and asks once for each reader: the reader is open, loaded, and playing; its book is a feed's item, not opened from the reading list; the rule continues to the next book and predownloadsNext; and timeRemaining is above 0 and at most predownloadLeadTime. It then asks LibraryViewModel.succession, and for an .episodeToDownload whose item is not downloading already (FeedItemState.isDownloading; one the newest-N rule put off, which waits for a network without data limits or for room, is fetched all the same, and this ask takes its place) it logs FEED_PREDOWNLOAD and asks FeedCenter.download(_:of:).
- How others observe it: the announcements through announceToVoiceOver(_:) and finishListening(announcing:), the wait through FeedEvent.downloadFinished, downloadFailed, and downloadCancelled, and the early download as any listener's download (the feed downloads' progress, in [Feeds](feeds.md)).

Search in Book on a transcribed audiobook, and Go Back (FR05, October 23, 2026): what the search reads, how a result is timed and chosen, and the jump's way back.

- Owner: ReaderViewModel, with the sheet's own BookSearchModel. canSearchText says whether there is a text to search: a text book that has loaded, or an audiobook whose content holds a transcript's text (any other audiobook has only spacing that counts time). searchCorpus copies what a search reads, so that it can run off the main actor: a text book's chapters with its headings, or the transcript's chapter texts, named by the audio chapters' titles alone, since an audiobook's headings count audio time, with a BookSearchTiming (Views/Reader/BookSearchView.swift: where each audio chapter begins, forTranscript(_:audioChapters:), and TranscriptText.chapterTime(atTextOffset:inChapter:)), so each BookSearchResult carries the time it is heard (time, and timedLabel for its row and its rotor entry). transcriptWithheld (a TranscriptTextWithheld) is why a book that has a transcript shows none of its text, set by the load (TranscriptTextLoader.Load.withheld, or editHeld when readContent holds a text edit for an audiobook) and cleared by showTranscript(_:); pendingJumpAnnouncement holds what to say once the sheet has closed. The way back is the reader's saved place (savedChapterIndex, savedCharacterOffset, hasSavedPosition), which every jump that goes through navigatePreservingReturnPosition(chapterIndex:characterOffset:) sets.
- Who may write it: ReaderPanelCommands (ReaderView.swift) takes the searchInBook notification: it toggles the sheet while canSearchText holds, and for a loaded audiobook that cannot be searched says why instead (announceNoTextToSearch(hasTranscript:), BookSearchWording.noTextToSearch, which names Transcribe Audio only for a book that offers it, and takes a reason the load found as proof that the book has a transcript, whatever TranscribedBooks has noted of it yet); the toolbar's Search button is there only while canSearchText holds. The sheet (ReaderPanelSheets.searchSheet) hands a chosen result to navigateToTranscriptSearchResult(chapterIndex:textOffset:) for a transcript, which converts the text offset to an audio offset (TranscriptText.audioOffset(atTextOffset:inChapter:)) and jumps through navigatePreservingReturnPosition, honouring Auto-play on navigate (autoPlayIfEnabled()), and sets pendingJumpAnnouncement (BookSearchWording.movedTo(time:location:)), which the sheet's onDismiss speaks through announcePendingJump(); navigateToSearchResult is a text book's. The goBack notification reaches the key Library window, for its folders, and the reader that takes it (shouldAcceptPanelCommand), which calls goBack(): returnToSavedPosition() while hasSavedPosition holds, otherwise BookSearchWording.nothingToGoBackTo. Return on a result row (an onKeyPress on the row's button, through choose(_:)) chooses the result, for every book.
- How others observe it: BookSearchView draws a timed result's row with its time as TimeFormatting.format, no Display picker (isTimed), and the field hint BookSearchWording.transcriptFieldHint; SearchResultRow's positionText and accessibilityDescription are internal, so tests read what a row says.
- Shared with the iPhone's Search in Book (0.4.4; the phone's side is in [The two apps and the platform folders](../platforms.md)), with the Mac's behavior unchanged:
  - The Mac's sheet hands a chosen result to goToSearchResult(chapterIndex:characterOffset:inTranscript:) (ReaderView.swift, searchSheet), which chooses between navigateToTranscriptSearchResult for a transcript and navigateToSearchResult for any other book. It is the one place that decides, so the phone's sheet moves the same way.
  - BookSearchWording (Views/Reader/BookSearchView.swift) now holds the sentences of the Mac's search that the phone shares, with their values unchanged: the search field's label "Search text" and its hints "Type to search within the book" and "Type to search the book's transcript", the switch's label "Use regular expression" and its hint "When enabled, search text is treated as a regular expression", the results label "Search results", and Back to Book's hint "Close search and return to the book".
  - noTextToSearch(bookTitle:canTranscribe:hasTranscript:withheld:offersTranscription:) and announceNoTextToSearch(hasTranscript:offersTranscription:) take offersTranscription, true by default. The Mac passes nothing and says what it said. With false, no sentence names Transcribe Audio or tells the listener to transcribe again: "<title> has no transcript to search." and the stale transcript's reason alone.
  - BookSearchModel takes a debounceInterval, init(corpus:debounceInterval:announce:). Its default, BookSearchModel.debounceInterval, is the Mac's 250 milliseconds, and the Mac's one call (BookSearchView) passes none.
