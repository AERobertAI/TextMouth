# Windows

Part of [TextMouth's architecture](../../../ARCHITECTURE.md).

- App-wide actions.
  - Owner: LibraryWindowRouter.shared. It keeps weak references to every open Library window, each with its way of taking an action, and to every reader window, each with its way of opening another reader, and decides once which window takes each GlobalAction: Open Book…, New from Text…, Import Folder…, Go to Library, opening files from Finder, opening a book's reader, and Continue to Next Book. An action that no open window can take waits while a Library window opens.
  - Who may write it: Library windows register and unregister themselves (ContentView); reader windows register (BookReaderWindowView); AppDelegate supplies the way to open a Library window. Menu commands, keyboard shortcuts, finished books, and imports that open their book (LibraryViewModel.readerOpener) post notifications that the router listens for on the default center from launch (GlobalAction.routedNotificationNames), and AppDelegate hands over the files Finder opens; each becomes one call of route(_:).
  - How others observe it: only the chosen window performs the action. While the library is unavailable or moving, a Library window refuses the actions that add or open books and says why, on its Library Not Available screen or in the move's sheet (DataDirectoryUnavailableText.refusal(of:) and DataDirectoryMoveWording.refusal(of:)); files opened from Finder during a move are imported once it ends.
  - Files: Services/LibraryWindowRouter.swift, Platform/macOS/App/AppDelegate.swift, App/MenuCommands.swift, Views/ContentView.swift, Views/Reader/BookReaderWindowView.swift.
- A reader window's book changing in place (0.2.5): listening goes on to the next book in the window you are in, and the registry of open readers.
  - Owner: the scene, the "Book Reader" WindowGroup(for: UUID.self) in Platform/macOS/App/TextMouthApp.swift, whose binding $bookID is the window's one book. It hands BookReaderWindowView a showBook closure that sets $bookID, and gives the view .id(bookID): a new value is a new view identity, so SwiftUI makes a new @StateObject ReaderViewModel in the same NSWindow and tears the old one down. The view stores the closure and gives it to its reader as nextBookWindowSwitcher as the reader is made (makeReader); a reader with no scene to set it in, as one made by a test, has none and always opens a new window. The registry of open readers is the library's: LibraryViewModel.openBookIDs, the books a reader is open for, counted per book (openReaderCounts). A reader registers its book as it is made (registerOpenBook(_:)) and unregisters it as it closes (unregisterOpenBook(_:)), and a book stays in openBookIDs until its last reader closes, so two readers of one book leave it open until both have closed. It is the one registry there is: the sleep timer's last-resort deadline asks it (SleepTimerService.heldTimerDeadline, in [Timers and statistics](timers-and-statistics.md)), and so does showNextBook, to tell whether the next book is open in another window.
  - Who may write it: the reader, in ReaderViewModel+NextBookWindow, once for every move to another book (the end of a book going on, Next and Previous in Reading List, Next and Previous in Folder): showNextBook(_:under:openingANewWindowWith:), which asks the pure NextBookWindowRule (Models/NextBookWindow.swift) what to do, and does it. The choice is NextBookWindowRule.choice(options:global:globalEndOfBook:): the feed's or plain folder's own ListeningOptions.nextBookWindow when it has one and its items go on (ListeningOptions.goesOn(under:); a dimmed choice is not in effect), else the global SettingsViewModel.nextBookWindow (SettingsPolicy.nextBookWindow, the config.json key nextBookWindow, defaulting to This Window); a move through the reading list passes no options and follows the global one (folderMoveWindowChoice, readingListMoveWindowChoice). NextBookWindowRule.action(choice:isBusy:canSwitch:nextBookIsOpenElsewhere:) gives .openANewWindow, today's post (.continueToNextBook or .openBookWindow), whenever the choice is A New Window, the reader has no switcher, or switchingTheWindowCouldLoseWork holds: the reader has closed (hasClosed) or its window is not on screen, a sheet or alert is attached, or a field editor is the first responder (windowStanding, a NextBookWindowStanding read from AppKit, which a test sets through windowStandingOverride; a minimized window, and every window of a hidden app, count as on screen), this book's text is being edited in another app (BookTextEditSessions.isEditing), or the library is moving or unavailable (dataDirectoryMove, dataDirectoryUnavailability); .switchThisWindow, which sets hasSwitchedWindow, leaves a WindowSwitchRequest for the book (WindowSwitchRequests.shared, a ReaderRequestStore), and calls the closure; and .bringTheOpenWindowForwardThenClose when the book is open in another window already, which posts as today and closes this reader's hostWindow on the next turn of the main queue, so no two windows show one book. The window choice is read when the book is asked for, never earlier, after every request for the book (autoplay, reading list) has been made, which the new reader claims once loaded.
  - How others observe it: BookReaderWindowView's onDisappear leaves the window registered with LibraryWindowRouter when the reader has switched its window (hasSwitchedWindow), and closes the reader as before; the new view registers the same NSWindow in onWindowFound, and registerReaderWindow(_:openReader:) replaces any earlier entry for a window, so an ordinary close unregisters as always. markBookOpened runs in the new view's onAppear, and reader windows are still not restored. The teardown of the old reader is its own: NowPlayingService, the media coordinator, and SleepTimerService key everything by readerID, so the old reader's close() clears, pauses, and unregisters only itself. A switched window is the same window, which nothing announces, so the reader that takes it over claims the switch request in attach(to:) once its window is found (a reader made for a new window finds none) and sees to the window's focus once its book has loaded (placeFocusAfterSwitchIfDue(), through windowAwaitingFocusAfterLoad): SwitchedWindowFocus.place(in:) notes StandardVoiceOverFocusTriggerView.focusPostCount(of:) as the load ends, and 1.6 seconds later (checkDelay) acts only in the key window and only when no trigger posted in that window since the load (shouldPlace(postsAtTheLoad:postsNow:windowIsKey:)), finding the element marked voiceOverFocusID and placing focus once (placeFocus). The play button's own trigger does the same when it runs, so VoiceOver is never told twice; that a switched window's focus lands as a new window's does has not been checked by hand with VoiceOver.
  - Files: Platform/macOS/App/TextMouthApp.swift, Views/Reader/BookReaderWindowView.swift, Models/NextBookWindow.swift, ViewModels/ReaderViewModel+NextBookWindow.swift, Services/WindowSwitchRequests.swift, Platform/macOS/Views/SwitchedWindowFocus.swift, Platform/macOS/Views/VoiceOverFocus.swift, Services/LibraryWindowRouter.swift, ViewModels/LibraryViewModel+BookOperations.swift, ViewModels/ReaderViewModel.swift.
- Each Library window's place.
  - Owner: the window's own views: its folder path, view, search, selection, and back and forward history (FolderNavigationHistory). A second Library window keeps its own. The view is a LibraryViewID (Models/LibraryViewDefinition.swift): one of the five categories, a LibraryCategory (ViewModels/LibraryViewModel.swift: Folders, All Items, Unopened, Recently Opened, and Reading List), the Feeds view, or a view the listener made, by its UUID, and the Library's views, their order, and their settings are in LibraryViewDefinitionStore (preferences, above). The window's identity of its view is ContentView's @State viewID, an optional that nothing saves: nil until the window first appears, when it takes the first view the pop-up offers (LibraryViewModel.shownView(_:in:center:), Folders as the views ship), and kept afterwards, even when the view is hidden in Settings or lists nothing, until the listener chooses another, so nothing moves under VoiceOver's cursor. LibraryView receives it as the binding shownViewID and makes a LibraryLocation of the view it names and the folder path (Models/LibrarySortState.swift), which every list, title, and sort of the window follows. A view deleted while the window shows it is no longer in the list: shownView falls back to the first view offered, LibraryView.followDeletedView sets shownViewID to it, and the window announces LibraryViewPickerWording.deletedView(nowShowing:), "The view this window showed was deleted. It now shows Folders." from itself. Unopened (LibraryCategory.unopened) replaced No Folder (noFolder), which listed the root's books, on October 7, 2026, and the type's decoder reads a category saved under No Folder's name as Unopened. LibraryCategory.recent is named "Recently Opened" since FR13 (October 10, 2026), in the picker and the window's title alike; the decoder reads the former name, Recent (formerRecentName), as it, and its sort is still saved under __recent__. Until October 30, 2026 the picker was a segmented control of the five categories, read as "Library category"; it is now a pop-up button, the View pop-up (LibraryView.viewPicker(offering:)), whose items are the names of the views LibraryViewModel.offeredViews(in:current:center:applicable:) offers, VoiceOver reading it as LibraryView.categoryPickerLabel, "Library view", the constant keeping the name the tests used, and its width bounded by LibraryViewPickerWording.pickerWidthLimit. Go to View 1 to Go to View 9 (ShortcutAction.goToView1, Command-1 to Command-9) and the Library menu's Go to View submenu post goToLibraryView with the number, which LibraryViewModel.goToView(numbered:in:folderPath:center:) answers in the window that takes commands.
  - Who may write it: the window's own navigation, and, for its folder, the library's folders changing elsewhere: ShownFolderFollowing (Views/Library/LibraryView.swift) notes, by ID, the folder the window shows each time it shows another, and when the library's folders change, as another window deletes, renames, or moves that folder, puts the window where FolderSheetNavigation.folderPath(showing:folderID:in:) says: the same path while a folder is there, the folder's new path when it is there under another, and otherwise the nearest folder above it still in the library, or the top level (the feed follow-ups after FR14). Nothing moves while the library's index is being recovered, when the list of folders is provisional.
  - Files: Views/ContentView.swift, Views/Library/LibraryView.swift, ViewModels/LibraryViewModel.swift, ViewModels/LibraryViewModel+Views.swift, Models/LibraryViewPickerWording.swift, Models/LibraryViewDefinition.swift, Models/LibrarySortState.swift, Models/FolderNavigationHistory.swift.
- The list a Library window shows.
  - Owner: LibraryView, which reads its list from the model once per draw (the lists Library windows show, above) and passes it down. Each row is a LibraryBookListRow, the unit SwiftUI compares: equal on everything it shows (the book, whether a reader has it open, the speed, edit mode and selection, the books its commands act on, the cloud state and commands, whether it is kept on this Mac, the manual-order moves, the removal preferences, the audio commands (LibraryAudioCommand) and the transcription note, the commands of the feed item it came from, whether it says its book is in the reading list, its reading list commands, and its book's live position object, compared by identity), never on its actions (LibraryBookRowActions), which read the current state when they run. So one changed book redraws one row, and SwiftUI builds a row's context menu only when the row has something new to show. A book resting at its end (Book.restsAtEnd, P07) says EndOfBookWording.finishedProgress, "Finished", on screen and to VoiceOver, in place of its percent, with no time left (BookRow.progressText(for:), spokenProgress(for:besideDownloadPercentage:), and timeRemaining(for:wpm:)), and Speak Info's facts take the row's words (BookInfoFacts, from LibraryInfoRow.swift). Move Book Up and Move Down come from the order the list shows (LibraryManualMoves), a folder's manual order or the reading list's. LibraryManualMove names a row's Move Up and Move Down once, for its context menu items and its VoiceOver actions; LibraryManualMoves.offered lists those a single book's row offers, only the moves its book can make, which BookRow puts first among its actions (FR09), running them through LibraryBookRowActions.moveSayingPlace, while the context menu's run moveInManualOrder. A window's two selections, the list's own and edit mode's checks, select together the listed books LibrarySelectionPolicy.selected(in:native:edit:) returns, in the list's order: those Move, Remove, and the reading list's commands act on (selectedBooks), which the edit bar (EditModeBar) and the Edit button's hint both count, folder and feed rows and books no longer listed left out (REV13). EditModeBar.offersDeselectAll(selectedCount:totalCount:) makes its Select All a Deselect All only while a book is listed and every one is selected, and Select All is dimmed while none is.
  - Files: Views/Library/LibraryView.swift, Views/Library/LibrarySubviews.swift.
