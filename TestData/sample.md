# The Art of Testing

This is a sample markdown document for testing TextMouth's markdown parser.

## Chapter One: The Beginning

Once upon a time, in a land of automated tests, there lived a parser that needed to prove itself worthy. It had to handle headings, paragraphs, and all manner of markdown formatting.

The parser was **bold** in its approach, and sometimes *italic* in its methods. It even handled `inline code` with grace.

### Section 1.1: Lists

Here are some things the parser must handle:

- Bullet point one
- Bullet point two
- Bullet point three

And numbered lists:

1. First item
2. Second item
3. Third item

## Chapter Two: The Middle

The story continues with more text to parse. This paragraph contains enough words to test word counting and speech rate calculations. We need several sentences here to make the test meaningful. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.

### Section 2.1: Special Characters

The parser must handle special characters like &amp; and <angle brackets> and "quotes" and 'apostrophes'.

## Chapter Three: The End
