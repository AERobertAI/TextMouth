Grade 2 sample: contractions used (UEB, ICEB Rules 2013)
==========================================================
Capital sign (dot 6, rule 8.2): The, We.
the        strong wordsign, dots 2346: "The" (twice).
and        strong wordsign, dots 12346.
with       strong wordsign, dots 23456.
can        alphabetic wordsign c, dots 14.
go         alphabetic wordsign g, dots 1245.
you        alphabetic wordsign y, dots 13456.
like       alphabetic wordsign l, dots 123.
ch         strong groupsign, dots 16: "church" (c-h-u-r-c-h = ch u r ch).
sh         strong groupsign, dots 146: "shop".
en         lower groupsign, dots 26: "open" (o p en).
ing        groupsign, dots 346, not at the start of a word: "singing" (s ing ing).
Uncontracted words: boy, girl, is.
Not exercised: lower wordsigns (be, his, in, was, were, enough), the dot-5 and dot-45 prefix
contractions, the shortforms, the numeric/grade 1 interplay, context rules for 'to into by'.
Each pattern is the one liblouis en-ueb-g2.ctb states (always/word/match rules) where the
file states it; the remaining cells follow ICEB Rules.  This file was NOT run through liblouis.
